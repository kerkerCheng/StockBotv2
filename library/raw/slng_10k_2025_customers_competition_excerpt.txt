【Item 1 Remote Power including for Data Centers】
During the fourth quarter of 2025, the Company concluded a multi-year contract for temporary remote power in Louisiana in accordance with its terms. The contract accounted for approximately 19% of 2025 revenues.

【Item 1 Remote Power Generation including Data Centers】
The Company believes that LNG offers speed to market and the ability to store significant volumes of natural gas in the event the data center is unable to access the electrical grid or natural gas delivered by a pipeline.

【Item 1 Competitive Strengths】
We believe we own one of the largest fleets of small-scale cryogenic transportation, storage, and vaporization equipment in North America.

【Item 1 Competition】
We also compete with other fuel sources including pipeline natural gas and compressed natural gas ("CNG").

【同節】
Location is often a primary competitive factor as transportation costs limit the distance LNG can be transported at competitive prices.

【Item 1A Risk Factors（資料中心長約）】
In the event, the data center is unable to properly integrate LNG into their operations, if we are unable to integrate additions of new equipment and personnel necessary to successfully deliver LNG to the customer, or if our sources of LNG are inadequate to service this contract, we may incur losses in cash flow and loss to our reputation related to future projects.
