【新聞稿 p.2 Key Developments for Q2 FY2027】
"Q2 FY27 Revenue from Operations grew 85% over the prior year, driven by volume growth, mix and higher copper prices."
"EBITDA in Q2 FY27 was Rs 897 million, up 95% from a year ago, while PAT of Rs 480 million increased 62% compared to Q2 FY26, and was 14% higher than Q1 FY27;"
"while EBITDA/Ton of approx. Rs 101,400 in Q2 FY27, improved 55% from Rs 65,500 a year ago and was driven largely by a meaningfully higher export contribution and better utilisation, together with a weaker Rupee and operating efficiencies;"

【新聞稿 p.3】
"Installed capacity on September 30, 2026 was 57,845 MTs, largely completing our Phase 2 expansion six -months ahead of schedule"
"In particular, export revenue surged 108% in Q2 FY27 compared to a year ago and 44% higher than Q1 FY27."
"As a result, exports ’ share of revenue improved to 32% in Q2 FY27, up from 26% in Q1 FY27;"
"positive operating cash flow of Rs 426 million during the first half of FY27"
"Consequently, debt-to-equity improved to 0.38x from 0.57x last quarter."
"During Q2, we received a new trial export order to supply Specialized wires for HVDC transformers"
"Meanwhile, last month Powergrid gave our Supa facility approval to manufacture specialized wires for 765kV transformers and reactors."

【新聞稿 p.2 管理層引言／p.3 New Capacity Expansion (Phase-3)】
"our Board has approved a Phase 3 expansion at Supa for Specialized wires that would result in total capacity increasing to 72,200 MTs by Q1 FY2029"
"The Company intends to spend up to Rs 1,850 million to finance this expansion, including the purchase of an additional 10 acres of land in Supa, along with building, plant and machinery, and will be funded through a combination of internal accruals and long-term debt."
