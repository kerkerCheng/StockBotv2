【開場：量與每噸 EBITDA 的原因｜p.4】
"Overall, sales volume was 8842 metric tons in Q2, was up from 7969 metric tons in Q1 and 7037 metric tons a year ago. This represents 26% year-over-year volume growth and 11% higher than Q1 of FY '27. Moving now to our EBITDA per ton performance: In Q2 FY '27, we reported EBITDA per ton of approximately Rs. 1 01,400 per metric ton up from roughly Rs. 93,000 per metric ton in Q1 and Rs. 65,500 per metric ton a year ago. Similar to Q1, the key drivers for this improvement were, number one being the CTC contribution during Q2 remained elevated given the timing of when that capacity was installed, though t he mixed percentage should moderate as we head into H2 of FY '27. Secondly, higher export contributions and volumes during Q2, which are all specialized Magnet Winding Wires, showed a meaningful improvement compared to last year and last quarter. The third point being an increase in consolidated utilization rates to 76% in Q2 of FY '27 versus 73% in Q1 of FY '27 based on available capacity for the quarter. Fourth point being a persistently weaker rupee"

【開場：出口｜p.3】
"Over the past few quarters, we have talked about having capacity available to expand our export business, given the supply constraints we faced previously. We spent the last 6 months fulfilling various technical qualifications and test shipments and in Q2, we saw the export volumes begin to increase from both existing customers as well as new OEMs. Export revenue in Q2 grew 108% year -over-year and 44% higher than last quarter. As a result, exports represented almost 32% of our revenue in Q2, up from 26% in Q1."

【開場：CTC 占比｜p.4】
"CTC contribution in Q2 remained elevated as a percentage of the total revenue as standard wire volumes begin to pick up late in the quarter. As the newly commissioned standard wire capacity ramps up, we expect the overall contribution of standard wires to increase and our product mix to progressively move closer to historical levels, heading into H2 of FY '27 and FY 2028."

【開場：特殊線材利用率｜p.5】
"If we look at our overall available specialized Magnet Winding Wire capacity, our Q2 utilization has exceeded 80%, which as we have previously mentioned is also an important threshold for when we start looking at adding new capacity."
