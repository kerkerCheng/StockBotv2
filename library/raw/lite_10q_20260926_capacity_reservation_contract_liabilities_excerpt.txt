MD&A（Components）：
"The Components products net revenue growth was primarily driven by the ramp of laser chip and laser assembly product shipment, which represent 66% of the total growth to support strong, broad-based demand across intra-data center, data center interconnect, and long-haul applications."

MD&A（Gross margin）：
"Gross margin for the three months ended September 26, 2026 increased to 51.0% from 34.0% for the three months ended September 27, 2025, primarily driven by higher revenue from our laser chip, laser assembly, and data transport products as well as our cloud transceiver product lines and optical circuit switch products. Approximately 42% of the gross margin dollar increase was driven by lower manufacturing costs as a percentage of revenue, primarily due to higher internal factory utilization. Additionally, 45% of the gross margin increase was driven by a mix shift to higher margin products."
