NOTE: 第三方轉錄（tier 2）；講者是 Amkor 執行長 Kevin Engel、財務長 Megan Faust——發行人自己的話（L8：供應商自述，不是客戶端證據）。用途：Amkor 敘事的完整重讀（2026-11-09）。①NVIDIA 那份南韓協議在 Amkor 這邊落在 HDFO（高密度扇出）的擴產：光州兩棟新廠房「including the recent announcement with NVIDIA」；②運算的成長來自旗艦 HDFO CPU 程式，兩個 2.5D 程式（網通、資料中心）第四季才上線——2.5D 還不在第三季的數字裡；③預付協議不只一家：2025 年以來十來家客戶；④亞利桑那第一期照進度 2027 年底完工、那位客戶在亞利桑那是 2028 年底的爬坡（反證第 1 條沒碰到）；⑤財務長：2027 年資產負債表穩、2028 年以後要不要新增借款還說不準（必須為真第④條）；⑥執行長說封裝正在變成主要瓶頸之一，但沒說是哪一種封裝（先進封裝層反證第 2 條沒碰到）。

【執行長開場：預付與南韓擴產（光州、松島）】
"Customers are not only planning further ahead, but are also making long-term commitments and providing advance payments tied to future capacity requirements."
"In Korea, we recently broke ground on 2 new buildings on our Gwangju campus. This additional space is being built to support the continued ramp of HDFO for our data center customers, including the recent announcement with NVIDIA."
"Following completion of these expansions in 2028, our Korea footprint is expected to increase by 40% from 2025."

【執行長開場：HDFO 與 2.5D 程式】
"Our flagship HDFO CPU program is scaling."
"An additional HDFO CPU program for a hyperscaler began ramping in Q3 and 2 new 2.5D programs in networking and data center are expected to come online in Q4."

【財務長：運算的第三季與第四季】
"During the quarter, we continued to scale our flagship HDFO CPU program and saw growth in other data center programs."
"Looking ahead to the fourth quarter, we expect revenue to increase low double digits sequentially, driven by ongoing scaling and new program ramps."
"Q4 is estimated to result in over 50% growth year-on-year and provides strong momentum heading into 2027."

【財務長：亞利桑那第一期與預付】
"Phase 1 construction remains on schedule for completion by the end of 2027."
"Since 2025, Amkor has executed advance payment arrangements with a dozen customers, primarily supporting advanced packaging capacity across both Korea and Arizona, and there are several more customer agreements under discussion."

【問答：南韓與亞利桑那的分工、資產可移轉（美銀分析師問 NVIDIA 協議）】
"Obviously, for Arizona, that ramp is later."
"So for that customer, that will be more of a 2020 -- late 2028 type ramp profile."
"The asset fungibility, we think about any high-density fan-out program, 2.5D, even wafer bumping type things, these assets are fungible up and down that stack."

【問答：第三季運算沒到原先說的季增三成（摩根士丹利分析師問是不是供給受限）】
"I wouldn't say anything related to supply constraints."

【問答：封裝是不是瓶頸、2.5D 程式的份量（瑞銀分析師問）】
"And I think that has highlighted even more that packaging is becoming one of the key bottlenecks."
"So customers are really working to make sure they secure capacity into '27 and even into 2028."
"I think we have programs going on in the xPU, networking programs and things like switches. Those are 2.5D type programs."
"But I think overall, the scale-wise, the big driver is the key programs we talked about."

【問答：運算 2026、2027 年的成長】
"I mean, so basically, with our guide, we're expecting over 30% growth in Computing from 2025 to 2026."
"We would expect a similar growth rate to even higher growth rate into 2027, again, based on that space profile that I outlined already."

【問答：2027 年資本支出與要不要再借錢】
"So just to recap there, we have the Arizona facility, we gave a framework that '27 could be up to 15% of that $12 billion in '27. And then for Korea in the range of $400 million to $450 million. And then Vietnam, $50 million. So altogether, those are higher than $2 billion just around those programs."
"So I would say it's still too early to tell whether we would need incremental debt within '28 and beyond. But I would say the balance sheet positioning is solid through 2027."
