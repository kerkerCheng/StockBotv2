【Note — Segment information（Data Centers 產品）】
The Data Centers segment is managed regionally and sells data center cooling solutions, including chillers, precision air handling units, computer room air conditioning and air handler units, fan walls, rear-door heat exchangers, coolant distribution units and immersion solutions.

【MD&A — Comparison of the three months ended September 30, 2026 and 2025（全公司）】
Second quarter net sales of $994.4 million were $255.5 million, or 35 percent, higher than the second quarter of the prior year, primarily due to $214.7 million of higher sales in our Data Centers segment, primarily driven by sales growth to hyperscale data center customers in North America.

【MD&A — Second quarter highlights（全公司毛利率）】
Gross margin declined 160 basis points to 20.7 percent, primarily due to lower gross margin in the Data Centers segment, largely driven by higher material costs.

【MD&A — Data Centers segment（三個月）】
Data Centers net sales increased $214.7 million, or 95 percent, from the second quarter of fiscal 2026 to the second quarter of fiscal 2027, primarily due to higher sales volume in North America, driven by sales growth to hyperscale customers.

【MD&A — Data Centers segment cost of sales（三個月）】
In addition, cost of sales was impacted by temporary operating inefficiencies, including the impact of supplier constraints for certain key components.

【MD&A — Data Centers segment gross margin（三個月）】
As a result of the higher sales and increased cost of sales as a percentage of sales, gross profit increased $31.7 million, or 58 percent, and gross margin declined 470 basis points to 19.7 percent.
