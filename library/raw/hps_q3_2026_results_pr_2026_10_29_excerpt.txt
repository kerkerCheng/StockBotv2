"GUELPH, Ontario, Oct. 29, 2026 (GLOBE NEWSWIRE) -- Hammond Power Solutions Inc. ("HPS" or the "Company") (TSX: HPS.A) a leading provider of magnetic and power electronic solutions, today announced its financial results for the third quarter 2026."
"Third quarter 2026 represents the first full quarter in which AEG's results are included in HPS's consolidated results."
"Beginning in the third quarter of 2026, following the acquisition of AEG Power Solutions ("AEG") on June 29, 2026, HPS began reporting its results through two reportable operating segments: Power Magnetics and Integrated Electrical Solutions ("IES")."
"Sales for the third quarter were $446,263, compared to $218,341 in the third quarter of 2025, an increase of $227,922 or 104.4%."
"Power Magnetics sales in the U.S. market were $288,728 in the third quarter of 2026, increasing 87.9% from $153,699 in the third quarter of 2025. The U.S. market continued to drive significant year-over-year growth, driven by increases in data centre shipments, along with modest improvements in industrial markets versus the prior year."
"The Company's Quarter 3, 2026 Power Magnetics backlog increased by 61.1% as compared to Quarter 3, 2025, primarily due to large project orders driven by data centre activity. Backlog decreased 3.8% from Quarter 2, 2026. While Quarter 3 2026 bookings were higher than in Quarter 2 2026, shipments (excluding AEG) were also higher by 12%, driving a decrease in backlog."
"Gross margin reached 34.4%, up from 30.1% a year ago, as pricing actions, higher factory output, more custom products and better efficiency helped us offset tariffs and rising copper and aluminum costs." (Richard Vollering, CFO)
"IES sales were $90,754 for the quarter made up of a mixture of end markets including oil and gas, power generation, and offshore wind. Other emerging markets with notable shipments in the quarter include nuclear and data centres."
