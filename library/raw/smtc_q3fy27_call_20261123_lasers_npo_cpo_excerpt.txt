雷射這一段來自 2026 年收購的 HieFo（InP 雷射）。三件事：①VHP 雷射（250 mW）放進 1.6T 收發器與 NPO 光引擎；②UHP 雷射（400 mW）配 SOA 瞄準 CPO scale-up 光源，營收在 2027 之後；③光子產能「gated by the capacity」，年底擴 3–4 倍、明年底再 3–4 倍，擴 3 倍時可支撐每季約 2,500 萬美元營收。

[法說 2026-11-23｜準備稿，雷射]
A single VHP laser can drive an 8-lane silicon photonics modulator, making it very well suited for 1.6T transceivers and NPO optical engines. We're also actively developing our ultra-high-power, or UHP, lasers with a 400-milliwatt of optical output. Paired with our semiconductor optical amplifier, this can address customer requirements for a CPO scale-up light source where the laser and amplification functions are central to system power and performance. Our photonics capacity expansion program is on track to support substantial demand for a differentiated product.

[法說 2026-11-23｜問答，CPO 與 NPO 時程（Hong Q. Hou，執行長）]
For CPO, we are developing CPO scale-up lasers, but that's a contribution of potential revenue beyond 2027. On the other hand, NPO, we do believe by the second half of 2027, we have a meaningful revenue contribution.
So NPO, we are very well positioned. CPO is a story for 2028 and beyond, and we'll see the meaningful revenue in the second half of FY '28.

[法說 2026-11-23｜問答，光子產能（Hong Q. Hou）]
Right now, we are gated by the capacity. So that's why we're increasing capacity, and we're on track, as I said, to expand the capacity by 3 to 4x by the end of this year and another 3 to 4x by the end of next year. By 3x increase, for example, end of this year, we will be having the capacity to support a run rate of $25 million a quarter. And of course, you can just scale that by the future capacity increase.

Can you talk about sort of where you stand versus your peers and your lead times? Is that driving a lot of the share gain? Hong Q. Hou: Yes. Tim, that's a good question. So our supply is -- we have been working on the supply issue. About 2 years ago, we actually -- our operations team, especially our Chief Operating Officer, saw that, and we went out and qualified additional fabs and secure more capacity. At this point of time, the foundry capacity is not a limitation. And on the back end, we are building the diversification and also the resiliency as well. For example, some of the testers will have a longer lead time and -- but it's going to be supporting our FY '28 plan.
So we view that as a differentiation because in the past, there are probably more competition and -- but now there are a few of us will have the capacity. And -- but this compounded with the growth -- with the performance and the qualification cycle since our product is already in the qualification with all module manufacturers, we know even for someone new to come in with a product, and they will have to go through the same process to get their customer and their customers qualified and even they have the security. So I'd say the combination of the performance, the road map alignment and secured capacity give us the confidence for the future growth trajectory.

[法說 2026-11-23｜問答，CPO 可能延後（分析師 Tristan Gerra 問、Hong Q. Hou 答）]
Tristan Gerra: Okay. And then as a follow-up, it looks like the CPO market could be delayed a bit. It looks like some hyperscalers are demanding a detachable connector for reliability purposes. Does that increase or extend the life of NPOs? And any commentary on the time line for CPO?
