[EXCERPT — 公開文件之有限逐字引文] Samsung Electro-Mechanics Press Release 2026-09-29：Sejong KRW 4.27 trillion · Vietnam subsidiary KRW 2.51 trillion FCBGA expansion
Source: https://www.samsungsem.com/global/newsroom/news/view.do?id=10562
Published: 2026-09-29 | Origin: Samsung Electro-Mechanics | Retrieved: 2026-10-04

摘要第一點："Investment of KRW 4.27 trillion to expand high-performance semiconductor substrate (FCBGA) production at the Sejong business site in Korea"
內文第 1 段："Samsung Electro-Mechanics is making a total investment of approximately KRW 6.78 trillion at its Sejong business site and Vietnam subsidiary to respond to the growing demand in the FCBGA (Flip Chip-Ball Grid Array) market for AI servers."
「Rapid Growth in Demand for High-Performance FCBGA Driven by AI Market Expansion」段："FCBGA is a highly integrated semiconductor substrate that connects high-performance semiconductors such as AI accelerators, GPUs, and CPUs to the mainboard, supplying electrical signals and power."
「Establishing FCBGA Production System to Meet Global Big Tech Customer Demand」段："In particular, this investment is being pursued based on mid- to long-term cooperation with global big tech and semiconductor customers. As the investment includes customer funding support and mid- to long-term volume guarantees, the company expects to strengthen the competitiveness of its FCBGA business based on a stable demand foundation."
摘要第二點："Global customer funding support resolves risks of new facility investment (CAPEX)"
