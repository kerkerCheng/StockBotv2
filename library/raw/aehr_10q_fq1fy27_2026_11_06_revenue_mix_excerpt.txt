【MD&A：營業現金流】
"Net cash used in operating activities increased by $2.8 million for the three months ended September 25, 2026, compared to the three months ended August 29, 2025. This increase in cash usage was primarily driven by an increase in accounts receivable associated with significantly higher revenue and increased cash used for prepaid expenses. The Company also increased inventory purchases in anticipation of higher customer demand; however, the cash impact was largely offset by a corresponding increase in accounts payable due to the timing of vendor payments. These uses of cash were partially offset by improved net income and an increase in cash provided by deferred revenue due to the timing of customer deposits and revenue recognition."
"Net cash used in operating activities during the one month ended June 26, 2026, was driven primarily by a net loss and cash used for inventory purchases in anticipation of higher customer demand, partially offset by cash provided by deferred revenue and collection of accounts receivable."
