【① 執行長 Thras Moraitis，磁材】
"In addition to the significant progress at Serra Verde, September saw us deliver another important achievement, our first commercial magnet sales from production at Stillwater, sintered NDFE block magnets to a high-performance material manufacturer. We also expect to sell finished magnet prototypes to multiple customers by the year-end. We have signed letters of intent and MOUs covering approximately 2,500 tonnes of magnets and secured purchase orders across the industrial, mobility, aerospace and defense sectors."
"Installation of Line B, which will double capacity is now underway and is expected to be commissioned in quarter 1 of next year. In September, we broke ground on our magnet facility in Blacksburg, South Carolina. Together with the full build-out of Stillwater, once online, Blacksburg is expected to bring our total U.S. magnet capacity to approximately 10,000 tonnes per annum."

【① 財務長，第三季營收】
"Revenue for the quarter, which represents sales to third parties was $3 million and included our first revenue from NdFeB magnet sales. As Thras noted, the decrease from the second quarter reflects industry-wide raw material shortages caused by China's export restrictions, most notably in heavy rare earths and Samarium metal. This limited what LCM and Stillwater could produce and sell."
