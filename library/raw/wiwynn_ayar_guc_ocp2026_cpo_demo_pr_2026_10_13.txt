[EXCERPT — 有限逐字引文] Wiwynn, Ayar Labs, and GUC Advance Co-Packaged Optics with Live Demonstration of Integrated Optical Compute System at OCP Global Summit 2026
（PR Newswire，SAN JOSE, Calif., Oct. 13, 2026；三家聯合新聞稿）

Wiwynn, an innovative cloud IT infrastructure provider for data centers, Ayar Labs, the leader in co-packaged optics (CPO) solutions for AI scale-up, and Global Unichip Corp. (GUC), the Advanced ASIC Leader, today announced the latest milestone in their strategic collaboration.
For the first time, they are presenting a live demonstration at OCP Global Summit 2026, showcasing Wiwynn's compute system integrating GUC's ASIC with Ayar Labs' TeraPHY™ optical engine paired with SuperNova™ light source, streaming real-time health telemetry.
The system integrates GUC's multi-chip packages (MCPs) with TeraPHY™ optical engines (OEs), powered by the Ayar Labs SuperNova™ light source.
Silicon-to-System Optical Integration: Wiwynn's system architecture integrates GUC's MCP with Ayar Labs' TeraPHY OEs paired with ELSFP-based high-power SuperNova light sources, into an ORV3-ready compute system.
This demonstration shows CPO has progressed from architectural validation to system-level functional proof, laying a solid foundation for hyperscale adoption. The three companies will continue deepening their collaboration, driving CPO from validation toward high-volume deployment.
