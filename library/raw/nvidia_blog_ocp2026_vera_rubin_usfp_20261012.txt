At OCP, NVIDIA and Cisco announced plans for USFP, a new optical pluggable transceiver module form factor designed to help AI factory builders scale next-generation AI fabrics while improving bandwidth density and power efficiency.
With approximately the same footprint as the widely adopted 200Gb/s-per-lane OSFP form factor, USFP will provide a path to 400Gb/s-per-lane networking and will extend a transceiver-based deployment model already proven across generations of data center networks.
The new form factor is designed for the scale and efficiency demands of next generation AI factories, supporting higher-bandwidth fabrics while preserving the flexibility and serviceability that have made pluggable optics a mainstay of modern data centers.
To help drive an open ecosystem, NVIDIA and Cisco are collaborating with TE Connectivity and Amphenol on cages, connectors and modules, with plans to advance USFP through a community-led, multi-source effort.
