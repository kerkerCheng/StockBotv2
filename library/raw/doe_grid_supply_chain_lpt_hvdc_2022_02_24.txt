報告 p.15（PDF 第 30 頁） 2.1.4.1——CTC copper（家數）："CTC copper: According to stakeholder outreach there are three total manufacturers producing CTC in North America, but only two companies in the United States producing CTC copper wire. However, through literature review, instead of two, three potential U.S. companies were identified: (1) Sam Dong, (2) Essex Furukawa, and (3) REA."
報告 p.15（PDF 第 30 頁） 2.1.4.1——CTC copper：Sam Dong："Sam Dong manufactures CTC at its Rogersville, Tennessee facility (Sam Dong, 2012)."
報告 p.15（PDF 第 30 頁） 2.1.4.1——CTC copper：Essex Furukawa（只掛 claim）："Essex Furukawa has multiple wire manufacturing facilities in the United States including Fort Wayne and Franklin in Indiana, and Franklin in Tennessee (Essex Furukawa, 2021), but it remains unclear if CTC can be produced at any of the domestic facilities."
報告 p.15（PDF 第 30 頁） 2.1.4.1——CTC copper：REA（只掛 claim）："Lastly, REA has four domestic manufacturing facilities including Fort Wayne and Lafayette in Indiana, Guilford in Connecticut, and Ashland in Virginia (REA, 2021). Literature has shown that the Fort Wayne, Indiana location possesses the capability to produce CTC copper wire (Gorman, 2008)."
報告 p.16（PDF 第 31 頁） 2.1.4.1——Insulating material（截在「for LPTs」：全句接「in the United States」，見 failure log #8）："Industry engagement indicated that insulating material was a potential supply concern due to a lack of domestic manufacturing. Additional industry stakeholders and literature review showed that there are only a few domestic suppliers for insulating material for LPTs"
報告 p.16（PDF 第 31 頁） 2.1.4.1——Insulating material：Weidmann："Weidmann manufactures part of its product line at its Urbana, Ohio facility (Weidmann, 2022)."
報告 p.16（PDF 第 31 頁） 2.1.4.1——Insulating material：Cindus："Cindus Corporation manufactures insulating material in its Cincinnati, Ohio facility (Cindus Corporation, 2022)."
報告 p.16（PDF 第 31 頁） 2.1.4.1——Insulating material：DuPont（stakeholder 轉述，只掛 claim）："DuPont was reported by a stakeholder to manufacture its Nomex insulating material in the United States."
