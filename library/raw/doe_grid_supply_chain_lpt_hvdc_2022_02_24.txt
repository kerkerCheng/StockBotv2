報告 p.20（PDF 第 35 頁）2.1.6 Transformer Supply Chain Bottlenecks in the United States："Currently, there are two major bottlenecks in the LPT supply chain. The first one is in the U.S. GOES production capacity. The second one is in the testing capacity of LPT manufacturers."
