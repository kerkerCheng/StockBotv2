[Soitec H1'27 新聞稿 2026-11-18｜anchor] Photonics-SOI revenue is now expected above $300m
High visibility on the remainder of FY27 enables Soitec to exceptionally extend its financial outlook to the full year, beyond its usual topline quarterly guidance. FY27 revenue is expected to be up around 40% y/y at constant currency and scope. Photonics-SOI revenue is now expected above $300m, more than 3 times its FY26 level. FY27 Gross Margin is expected slightly above 30%, with H2’27 impacted mainly by temporary higher process costs related to the pivot of our manufacturing setup to increase Photonics-SOI capacity. FY27 CAPEX cash-out is now expected around €130m, up from the previous indicative guidance of around €100m, to increase Photonics-SOI production capacity. FY27 Free Cash Flow is expected to be around €25m, reflecting FY27 CAPEX phasing, the Photonics-SOI ramp-up in H2’27 and beyond, as well as around €40m outflow from the settlement of a tax dispute.

[Soitec H1'27 新聞稿 2026-11-18｜anchor] To serve this demand, Soitec is now expanding its capacity in three areas
This demand is increasingly secured by firm customer commitments, with most of our Photonics-SOI customers having now signed capacity reservation agreements, backed by cash deposits. These contracts amount to customer commitments of approximately $1.2bn for the coming years, and additional volumes for 2028 and beyond are under discussion. To serve this demand, Soitec is now expanding its capacity in three areas: increasing Photonics-SOI output within existing fabs in FY27, repurposing the Bernin 4 cleanroom, and equipping our Singapore extension in modular steps, in line with customer demand. For FY27, we continue to expect contrasting dynamics across our end markets: Mobile Communications: progress in POI should offset the ongoing customer inventory correction in RF-SOI, in a challenging smartphone market Edge & Cloud AI: momentum keeps strengthening, driven by Photonics-SOI Automotive & Industrial: long-term customer agreements provides good visibility but early signs of end-market recovery are not expected to benefit Soitec this year.
