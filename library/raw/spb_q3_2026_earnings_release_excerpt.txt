【p.3 CNG】
"CNG Adjusted EBITDA (1) increased $10.8 million, or 42%, to a record $36.5 million, driven by continued growth in its industrial and data center business and increased wellsite activity partially offset by higher operating costs"

"The pipeline for wellsite, industrial, data center and Mobile CNG Fleet FuelingTM work is expected to propel continued gains into 2027"

【p.3 Balance Sheet and Capital Allocation】
"The increase reflects the reprioritization of capital to investments in the CNG segment, driven by rapidly developing opportunities in power generation for data center customers and growth in the transportation market."
"Leverage Ratio(1) of 3.8x compared to 3.9x a year ago and 4.0x at prior year-end, reflecting lower net debt balances, partially offset by lower Adjusted EBITDA(1)"

【p.4 2026 Outlook】
"Anticipated 2026 CNG Adjusted EBITDA growth has improved to ~5% driven by stronger-than-expected growth in the data center and industrial segments coupled with continued strength in the wellsite business. This growth is not expected to offset a decline in propane, driven by capacity investments to improve winter readiness as well as temporarily elevated customer attrition following last winter."
