"CPO — Co-Packaged Optics Optical engines integrated directly onto the switch-ASIC package substrate. Advantages ● Shortest electrical path → lowest insertion loss & best signal integrity ● Fewest interfaces / RF reflection points → most scalable to higher lane rates (200G → 400G+) ● System-level RAS partially proven through 51.2T CPO ● Simpler accountability model than NPO Challenges ● Less modular — optical engines are typically soldered to the package and tied to the ASIC vendor's implementation ● Higher optics supply chain risk"

【Meta 投影片〈Integrated Optics at Scale: From 51.2T Validation to Future 102.4T Product〉（OCP Global Summit 2026, Special Focus: Photonics；Granthana Rangaswamy, Siamak Amiralizadeh, Xu Wang, Meta；投影片無日期，日期取同場 OCP 官方影片上架日 2026-10-14〔會期 2026-10-12～15〕；pypdf 抽字，表格欄位照抽字順序）：p.8〈Integrated Optics Implementation Trade-offs〉NPO 欄】
"NPO — Near-Packaged Optics Optical engines mounted near the ASIC linearly driven from the SerDes. Advantages ● Modular interposer approach can foster a broader multi-vendor ecosystem ● Comparable power savings to CPO at 102.4T switch bandwidth (200G/lane) ● Socketed options improve manufacturing flexibility / reworkability. ● Enables integrated and pluggable optics mixed Switch design, which can be required for some network deployments. Challenges ● Longer traces + extra connectors → increased signal integrity and TP2/TP3 compliance risk at scale w/o retimers ● Linear-drive viability degrades at higher lane rates (e.g., 448G/lane); retimers may be needed, eroding the advantage toward pluggable levels. RAS needs to be proven: new mechanics & failure modes"
