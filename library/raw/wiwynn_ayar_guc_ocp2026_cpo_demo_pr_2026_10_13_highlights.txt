"Live Demonstration Highlights: Silicon-to-System Optical Integration: Wiwynn's system architecture integrates GUC's MCP with Ayar Labs' TeraPHY OEs paired with ELSFP-based high-power SuperNova light sources, into an ORV3-ready compute system. OE Telemetry and System Health Monitoring: Real-time optical engine telemetry gives data centers visibility into system status, strengthening reliability at scale. Stability Metrics Under Live Operation: As the tuning subsystem tracks thermal and wavelength drift (TX heater activity), key eye metrics (eye height, OMA, ER) hold steady alongside OE and ELSFP temperatures. Advanced Cooling Solution: Wiwynn's system-level cooling architecture supports stable operation of optical power under real-world conditions. Detachable FAU and In-Chassis Fiber Routing: Demonstration of serviceability and manufacturability at the compute system level."
