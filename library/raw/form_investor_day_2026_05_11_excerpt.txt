【FormFactor 投資人日（2026-05-11）逐字稿，第三方轉錄（StockAnalysis 轉載 Quartr，tier 2；後半已用本機聽寫對過 Quartr 錄音 3570–3606 秒）：Jens Klattenhoff（Systems 事業群總經理）——insertion 1 在 300mm 晶圓上測 PIC】
"At insertion one, testing the photonic IC on a 300 mm wafer is where FormFactor operates and leads. We are catching defects very early in the process before high value components are getting added to the package. Testing here at this point reduces cost of good die by up to 5 times compared to other insertions. We also accelerate the yield learnings and can isolate photonic issues from assembly and production effects. Later insertion exists, they do not scale, at least currently. High complex test systems, long cycle times, low throughput are not economically yet. FormFactor focus on insertion one today."
