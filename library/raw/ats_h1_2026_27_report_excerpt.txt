p.4（Business developments and situation）："Consolidated revenue rose from € 846.3 million in the comparative period to € 1,230.6 million in the first half of 2026/27 (increase of € 384.3 million or 45.4 %). In the Microelectronics segment, revenue was € 347.3 Mio. €, or 82.9 %, higher than in the prior-year comparative period. The positive development was driven by demand for IC substrates and the start of production at the new plant in Kulim, Malaysia."
p.4 表：EBITDA 409.0（PY 174.7）；EBITDA margin 33.2%（PY 20.6%）；EBIT 226.4（PY 0.0）；Profit for the period 166.2（PY (63.5)）；Earnings per share (€) 3.98（PY (1.86)）。

p.5（Business development by segments）："The share of the Electronics Solutions segment in total third party revenue decreased from 56.7% to 37.7%. The share of the Microelectronics segment in revenue rose to 62.3% (previous year: 43.3%)."

p.6（Microelectronics segment）："The segment's revenue amounted to € 766.4 million, up by € 347.3 million or 82.9% above the prior-year revenue of € 419.1 million. This increase is attributable to higher volumes, higher prices, and the expansion of production in Kulim, Malaysia, and Leoben, Austria."
p.6："EBITDA improved by € 256.9 million or 324.7% from € 79.1 million to € 336.0 million." "Overall, this resulted in an EBITDA margin of 43.8%, which is higher than in the previous year (18.9%)."

p.19（Hybrid capital）："In June 2026, AT&S AG issued a hybrid convertible bond in the amount of €400.0 million, which was recognized in equity net of transaction costs, since this bond has an indefinite maturity and there is no contractual obligation to repay it on a specific date."
