"Information about major customers
During the financial year ended December 31, 2025, three customers each accounted for more than 10 percent of the Group’s total revenue. Revenue from these customers amounted to SEK 183.4 million (2024: three customers, SEK 96.5 million).
Customer A: SEK 79.4 (75.3) million
Customer B: SEK 55.2 (16.9) million
Customer C: SEK 48.7 (4.4) million
These revenues were attributable to the following operating segments:
Customer A: Wireless
Customer B: Wireless
Customer C: Wireless
The Group does not disclose the identity of major customers."
