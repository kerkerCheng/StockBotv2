[press release, opening paragraph] Hyundai Mobis announced that it has formed a strategic collaboration with Boston Dynamics, a global leader in robotics, at CES 2026 in Las Vegas.

[press release, opening paragraph] The company stated on the 7th that it will supply actuators for Atlas, Boston Dynamics’s next-generation humanoid robot.

[press release, opening paragraph] the collaboration with Hyundai Mobis will help accelerate Boston Dynamics and HMG’s mass production plans for Atlas.
