Trading Update

- Further uplift of FY 2026 revenue guidance to in excess of 40% growth year-on-year

IQE plc (AIM: IQE, "IQE" or the "Group"), the leading global supplier of compound semiconductor wafer products and advanced material solutions, today provides a trading update for the year ending 31 December 2026.

IQE is continuing to maintain strong momentum across all core segments during the second half of 2026. In addition, revenue upside opportunities identified in optical communications for datacentre and AI infrastructure markets have now materialised.

As a result, IQE now expects revenue growth and adjusted EBITDA to be ahead of previously upgraded expectations, resulting in year-on-year revenue growth in excess of 40% and adjusted EBITDA in the mid-teens (£m).

The Group remains bank-debt free with a current cash position of £41.5m.

Jutta Meier, Chief Executive Officer of IQE, commented:

"The continued strength of our performance reflects IQE’s critical role in enabling the rapidly growing AI and datacentre infrastructure market. The conversion of previously identified opportunities into revenue while maintaining disciplined operational execution across the Group has enabled us to upgrade our full-year outlook once again. With strong order book coverage already in place for 2027, we are well positioned to build on this momentum and capitalise on the opportunities ahead."
