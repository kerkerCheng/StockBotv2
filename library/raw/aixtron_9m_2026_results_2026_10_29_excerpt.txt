"Optoelectronics accounted for roughly 68% of our equipment order intake in the third quarter, reflecting exceptionally strong demand for AI-driven optical connectivity. With an equipment order backlog of EUR 549.4 million, we have revenue visibility well into 2027. At the same time, improving customer utilization, renewed order activity and preparations for new capacity provide increasing evidence of a recovery in GaN and SiC power electronics. Together, these trends reinforce our expectation of a strong growth year in 2027, led by optoelectronics,"
