【p.1 CEO Review】
"Production of 2,082 tonnes of NdPr and 23 tonnes of Dy and Tb was achieved during the quarter. This production result reflects the work of the operations team to overcome ore concentrate quality challenges that affected downstream processing during the June 2026 quarter."
"Quarterly sales revenue of A$310.1m was 55% higher than in the prior corresponding period (Q1 FY26) and is the highest quarterly sales revenue achieved since Q4 FY22. The average selling price across all rare earth products during the period was a record A$102.1/kg. The 88% increase in average selling price compared to the prior corresponding period (Q1 FY26) continues to be driven by improved market pricing, increased bundling of light and heavy rare earths for magnet customers, and a further increase in the premium achieved above the market index, including as a result of floor price agreements which now cover over 50% of Lynas' production."
"This included progress on construction of the expanded Heavy Rare Earths (HRE) facility at Lynas Malaysia, following the commencement of production of Dy in May 2025, Tb in June 2025 and Sm in March 2026. Construction activities are currently focused on concreting works for the Solvent Extraction, Motor Control Centre and Control Room buildings. Procurement of long lead items is progressing and the project is being sequenced to prioritise production of Gadolinium and then Yttrium."

【p.2 JS Link／LS Eco Energy】
"Lynas will supply rare earth materials to the JS Link magnet factory in Yesan, South Korea, and the planned factory in Malaysia at commercial prices under an exclusive supply arrangement until January 2038."
"Lynas and LS Eco Energy continue to work towards the definitive agreement for the long-term metal processing arrangement, including HRE metal making, covering the use of Lynas' rare earth products at a metal making facility to be constructed by LS Eco Energy in Vietnam."
