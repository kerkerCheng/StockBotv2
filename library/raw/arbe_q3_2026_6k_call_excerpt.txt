(NASDAQ: ARBE) (TASE: ARBE), a global leader in ultra-high-resolution radar solutions, today announced financial results for its third quarter ended September 30, 2026. Based on current market conditions and customer engagement visibility, the Company’s outlook for 2026 is as follows: ● Revenue in the range of $2.3 million to $3.0 million. The revised range reflects the timing of two integration projects, for which deliveries and related revenue recognition were originally expected in the fourth quarter of 2026 and have shifted to 2027. ● Adjusted EBITDA for 2026 is projected to be a loss in the range of $34 million to $35 million. The revised range reflects lower expected revenue for 2026 and the Company’s strategic investment in building a production line for radar systems and in building inventory to position the Company for potential order growth in the coming year.

[法說逐字稿 2026-11-19（Motley Fool）｜anchor] Series-production radar deliveries are planned to start in the fourth quarter of 2027, ahead of the start of production
In September, together with HiRain, our Tier 1 in China, we announced that one of the world's largest automotive groups selected HiRain's imaging radar for a Level 3 passenger vehicle program. This program covers multiple brands within the group. HiRain will make, supply, and integrate the full radar system, which is based on our chipset. Series-production radar deliveries are planned to start in the fourth quarter of 2027, ahead of the start of production. This win came after a long, detailed evaluation. The OEM tested our technology, safety, quality, and commercial readiness. For Arbe, this is an important milestone and significant validation from the market, and it gives us clear paths to automotive mass production over the long term. Our immediate focus now is on execution. We are working closely with our Tier 1, HiRain, to meet every step on the way to production.

[法說逐字稿 2026-11-19（Motley Fool）｜anchor] In robotaxi, our work with a leading global robotaxi player continues to progress
This gives us a strong position in China through a Tier 1 with the scale and the customer relationships to take our technology into mass production. Beyond China, we continue to work with global OEMs and robotaxi players that are testing advanced radar for Level 3 and Level 4 autonomy. In robotaxi, our work with a leading global robotaxi player continues to progress. After initial sales, then follow-on orders for our Phoenix radar systems, the vehicles are now driven on U.S. roads with our radars giving full 360-degree sensing for Level 4 autonomy. They are collecting data from our radar to train their driving model, and they are building their perception stacks based on our output. This is an important step. Once the customer trains its system on our data, our radar becomes part of the core design.
