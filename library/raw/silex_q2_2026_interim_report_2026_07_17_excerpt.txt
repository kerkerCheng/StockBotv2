p.3 CEO comments（Edvard Kälvesten）：
"We continue to see stable and growing demand across all Silex’s geographic and end markets. Customer interest was particularly strong for optical switches in data centers within the end market telecom in North America."
"After the end of the quarter, and two months after the listing on Nasdaq Stockholm, Silex entered into a binding agreement with the U.S. semiconductor manufacturer Onsemi to acquire a semiconductor fab in Mountain Top, Pennsylvania, USA."
"This is an important step in our strategy to get closer to our largest customers, expand our production capacity, shorten lead times and reduce geopolitical exposure."
"The objective is for the facility to reach EBIT break-even between 2029 and 2030 and to achieve revenue and profitability levels comparable to those of Silex's Swedish 200 mm facility in 2025 by 2034."
"The expansion of the cleanroom in Järfälla is progressing according to plan and is expected to increase capacity by approximately 35%."

p.4 Group performance, net sales：
"Net sales from development projects increased by SEKm 61 compared with the corresponding quarter last year, while production projects accounted for SEKm 23 of the total increase."
"Continued strong demand for AI and optical switches among the Company's customers contributed to growth in the telecom end market, which accounted for SEKm 34, or 34.5%, of the total increase."
"Geographically, growth was primarily driven by North America, where net sales increased by SEKm 70 compared with the corresponding quarter last year."
