[10-Q Q3 2026｜anchor] Gross profit as a percentage of sales decreased to 32.2%
Gross profit increased $65.5 million, or 101.4%, to $130.1 million in the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared with $64.6 million in the three months ended September 30, 2025. The increase in gross profit is primarily attributable to the increase in sales volume as described above. Gross profit as a percentage of sales decreased to 32.2% in the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared with 32.7% in the three months ended September 30, 2025. Gross profit for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was reduced by $16.7 million of additional cost of sales related to the fair value adjustment to inventory acquired in the Thermon acquisition. Adjusted gross profit margin was 36.3% for the three months ended September 30, 2026.
