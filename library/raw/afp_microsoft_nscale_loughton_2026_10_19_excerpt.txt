【AFP 2026-10-19（Courthouse News 轉載）：Microsoft 證實退出 Nscale 英國 Loughton 案，發言人說「this decision relates to a single site」；Nscale 發言人說倫敦電網問題不影響其他合約；知情人士說占 Microsoft 對 Nscale 合約的一小部分；UKPN 不評論個案、說許多資料中心在等國家輸電網工程（媒體轉述，非一手）】
"(AFP) — Microsoft confirmed Monday that it has pulled out of a U.K. AI data center deal with British startup Nscale, in a blow to the country’s artificial intelligence ambitions. The U.S. technology giant agreed last year to lease computing capacity from AI infrastructure provider Nscale, which is backed by chip giant Nvidia, at a planned data center in Loughton, northeast of London. A Microsoft spokesperson said “this decision relates to a single site,” adding that the company remains “committed to our partnership with Nscale.” The deal was announced alongside a flurry of multibillion-dollar investments from U.S. tech giants during President Donald Trump’s state visit to the United Kingdom last year. The investments were touted as helping turn Britain into an AI superpower. Former Prime Minister Keir Starmer cited the Loughton site as the “largest UK sovereign AI data centre.” But some data center projects have struggled to get off the ground because of concerns over high electricity prices and lengthy waits to connect to Britain’s power grid. “We remain committed to both the Loughton project and the UK and we are exploring alternative power sources for the site, in addition to other expansion sites in the UK,” an Nscale spokesperson said Monday. “Our relationship with Microsoft remains strong and long-term, and this unfortunate London-wide grid issue has no impact on our other contracts,” the spokesperson added. British media reported that power grid officials had told Nscale the site would not have access to power until the 2030s. Wall Street IPO The withdrawal comes as Nscale prepares for an initial public offering on the New York Stock Exchange, reportedly targeting a valuation of up to $35 billion. In its IPO filing, Nscale noted that “a substantial portion of our committed revenue is driven by a limited number of customers with large contracts,” namely Microsoft and U.S. AI giant Anthropic. The Loughton deal accounted for only a small portion of Microsoft’s agreements with Nscale, according to a person familiar with the matter. Earlier this year, ChatGPT maker OpenAI withdrew from a partnership with Nscale and Nvidia to establish a U.K. version of Stargate — a large-scale AI infrastructure initiative. OpenAI blamed high energy costs and regulatory uncertainty for shelving the project, according to British media. Meeting the electricity demands of large new data centers often requires major infrastructure upgrades, adding to connection delays. UK Power Networks, which does not disclose information about individual connections, told AFP on Monday that “many data centres, whilst they can connect to our distribution network, are awaiting works on the national transmission network, which we do not own.”"
