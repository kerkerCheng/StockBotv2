【CEO 引言（power 段）】
"Liberty Power Innovations (“LPI”) entered into a strategic development agreement with a leading frontier AI lab and a dedicated infrastructure platform for 1.2 GW of integrated power services at data center campuses."

【同段下一句】
"The agreement includes a significant prepayment of capacity fees and targeted execution of the first energy service agreement (“ESA”) contract for approximately 500 megawatts ("MW") by December 2026."

【同段】
"The agreement also establishes a phased energization schedule from Q4 2027 through Q1 2029 and includes the substantive commercial terms for the additional long-term ESAs."

【同段 CEO 引言】
"We see significant opportunity to expand this relationship and are already working together to advance additional projects across other sites, including gas supply, interconnection, permitting and construction planning."
