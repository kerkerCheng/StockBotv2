"Supermicro’s In-Row CDU adopts a liquid-to-liquid heat exchanger design to deliver exceptional cooling performance for high- power, high-density server clusters. With outstanding thermal transfer efficiency and a total cooling capacity of up to 2.2 MW, it can support multiple racks or entire clusters within a facility. When integrated with Supermicro’s Water Cooling Tower, it maximizes overall efficiency and ensures long-term operational reliability for large-scale deployments."
