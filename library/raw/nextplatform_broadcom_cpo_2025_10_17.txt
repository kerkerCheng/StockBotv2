[Meta Bailly CPO reliability testing]
"over the first 1 million device hours...there were zero link flaps observed."
"the highest device hours reported for CPO technology operation at the system level" with "no failures or uncorrectable codewords (UCWs) in the links."

[Power efficiency]
An 800 Gb/sec Bailly port consumed 5.5 watts compared to 16-18 watts for standard pluggable optics. Meta's paper noted CPO delivered "65 percent lower power per 100 Gb/sec lane compared to retimed pluggable optical models."

[Davisson / Tomahawk 6 CPO]
Third-generation device: 102.4 Tb/sec capacity with field-replaceable laser modules. An 800 Gb/sec port consumes approximately 3.5 watts — 36.4% lower than Tomahawk 5 CPO and over 70% less than pluggable optics.

[Hyperscaler adoption]
Meta Platforms deployed/tested Bailly-based CPO switches (Tencent presumed by the author, not confirmed).
