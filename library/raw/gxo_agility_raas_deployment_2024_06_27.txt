Excerpt:
[announcement, opening paragraph] GXO … and Agility Robotics … announced today that they have signed a multi-year agreement to begin deploying Digit in GXO’s logistics operations.

[announcement, deployment description] As part of the RaaS agreement, GXO is deploying Digit robots and Agility Arc™, Agility’s cloud automation platform for deploying and managing Digit fleets.

[announcement, SPANX facility use case] Digit robots are assisting with repetitive tasks such as moving totes from cobots and placing them onto conveyors, all orchestrated through Agility Arc.

[announcement, RaaS paragraph] Under the RaaS agreement, the companies will continue to explore additional use cases and scale Digit usage to meet demand throughout the deployment.
