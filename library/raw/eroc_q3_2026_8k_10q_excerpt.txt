【8-K｜Business Highlights】
"Increased revenue nearly 4x quarter-over-quarter driving positive net income of $8.9MM and Adjusted EBITDA* of $12.6MM, due to increased generator production and installation activity."
"Began construction on schedule for the 366 megawatt (MW) El Paso Electric generation facility, supporting Meta's data center campus."
"Expanding manufacturing capacity by 900 MW/year with the new Atlas facility, bringing run-rate capacity to 2.1 gigawatts (GW) per year in 2028."
"Added power system sales bookings of over $300 million after quarter close, pushing backlog to a new record."
"Ended the quarter with strong liquidity, including $549 million of unrestricted cash, no outstanding debt and an undrawn $250 million credit facility as of September 30, 2026."

【10-Q｜在手】
"Contracted Power System Sales Backlog as of September 30, 2026 was approximately $1.6 billion, an increase of $1.5 billion, as compared to September 30, 2025. This increase was driven by new contracts with data center customers, partially offset by net reductions in backlog from utility and commercial and industrial ("C&I") customers as revenue recognized during the period exceeded new bookings in those customer groups."
"Substantially all backlog growth year-over-year was attributable to contracts with data center customers, reflecting strong demand for speed-to-power solutions to support accelerated data center development timelines and increasing power requirements associated with artificial intelligence ("AI") and digital infrastructure. Geographically, the backlog growth year-over-year was driven predominantly by projects located in Texas…"
