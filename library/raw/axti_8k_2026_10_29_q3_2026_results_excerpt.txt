【CEO 引言】
"Our revenue in Q3 reached a record $73.4 million, up 54 percent from the prior quarter and up 162 percent year over year, driven by exceptionally strong customer demand for our indium phosphide material."
"We entered 2026 with a commitment to double our indium phosphide capacity and I am pleased to report that not only have we surpassed that goal but we have more than quadrupled our 2026 indium phosphide revenue opportunity through additional productivity gains and the expansion of our capabilities for high-value, large diameter products."

【第三季數字】
"Revenue for the third quarter of 2026 was $73.4 million, compared with $47.6 million for the second quarter of 2026 and $28.0 million for the third quarter of 2025."
"GAAP gross margin was 56.0 percent of revenue for the third quarter of 2026, compared with 44.9 percent of revenue for the second quarter of 2026 and 22.3 percent for the third quarter of 2025."
"GAAP net income, after minority interests, for the third quarter of 2026 was a net income of $25.7 million, or $0.39 diluted income per share, compared with a net income of $11.1 million, or $0.17 per share, for the second quarter of 2026 and a net loss of $1.9 million, or $0.04 per share, for the third quarter of 2025."
