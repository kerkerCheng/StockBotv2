[EXCERPT — 有限逐字引文] Credo Technology Group Holding Ltd Form 10-K（fiscal year ended 2026-05-02）Item 1 Business — Optical DSPs 節錄：光 DSP 已到第七代；100G/lane 三代各有 LRO 專用的單向 8×100G 版本；200G/lane Bluebird 支援全重定時與 LRO 的 1.6T 收發器
DATE: 2026-06-15
RETRIEVED: 2026-10-20（原檔本機抽字、空白攤平後逐字核對）
NOTE: Credo 自己的法定文件（自述）。「pioneered by Credo」是自稱。本節沒有 LRO 出貨量、營收占比或客戶。

【Item 1. Business — Optical DSPs 第一段（10-K 頁碼 11）】
"Credo optical digital signal processors (DSPs) are core components within optical transceivers deployed in AI clusters, hyperscale data centers, service‑provider networks, enterprise networks, and 5G wireless infrastructure."

【Item 1. Business — Optical DSPs 第二段】
"Now in their seventh generation, Credo DSPs operating at 50G/lane, 100G/lane and 200G/lane PAM4 signaling combine high performance, low latency, power efficiency, and high reliability."

【Item 1. Business — Optical DSPs，Dove／Lark／Robin 段首句】
"The Credo Dove, Lark, and Robin families operate at 100G/lane across three successive technology generations."

【Item 1. Business — Optical DSPs，Dove／Lark／Robin 段末句】
"Each generation also offers a unidirectional 8×100G variant designed for Linear Receive Optics (LRO), an architecture pioneered by Credo that removes DSP functionality from the module receiver to improve energy efficiency in large‑scale AI deployments."

【Item 1. Business — Optical DSPs，Bluebird 段】
"Bluebird is designed to enable a new generation of full‑retimed and LRO‑based 1.6 Tb/s transceivers, delivering high performance, low power consumption, and high reliability."
