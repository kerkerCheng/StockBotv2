[CEO prepared remarks — FY26 accomplishments]
"First, our fab engineers recently made significant progress improving the quality of our indium phosphide lasers facet, allowing our lasers to better address CW applications."
"These results support MECOM's strategy to enter the multi-billion dollar CW laser market, which we are targeting for 2028."

[CEO prepared remarks — data center]
"As we look ahead, we expect to produce significant volumes of 200G PDs as well as 400G PDs and, as I previously mentioned, CW lasers for 800G, 1 .6T, and 3 .2T applications."

[Q&A — manufacturing footprint; CEO answer on Ann Arbor]
"They are really geared towards, I would say, low and medium volume, indium phosphide."
"They have an MBE reactor, and they are able to develop their own epi, and that's really one of the key technologies that we bring to our PD product line."
