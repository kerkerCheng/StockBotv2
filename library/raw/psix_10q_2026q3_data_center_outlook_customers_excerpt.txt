【MD&A 營收】
"Net sales decreased $1.8 million, or 1%, during the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to the three months ended September 30, 2025, as a result of lower sales of $6.2 million and $1.1 million in the power systems and transportation end markets, respectively, partially offset by an increase of $5.6 million in the industrial end market."
"Sales in the power systems end market declined primarily due to softness in demand within the oil and gas market partially offset by higher sales of data center related products."
"The Company continues to see strong demand for data center power solutions; however, the timing and ultimate volume of revenue recognized from that demand remain subject to customer scheduling, manufacturing throughput, supply chain factors and other variables, and the Company is not predicting any specific level of data center revenue in any future period."

【MD&A 展望（Recent Trends and Business Outlook）】
"Based on the current production schedule and information available as of the date of this Quarterly Report, the Company is raising its outlook and now expects second-half 2026 net sales to exceed second-half 2025 net sales, as larger Power Systems orders move into production and are recognized as revenue."
"The Company previously expected second-half 2026 net sales to be approximately in line with second-half 2025 net sales."
"including current material availability constraints affecting certain planned fourth quarter deliveries; there can be no assurance that orders will convert to revenue in any particular quarter."
"Softness in demand within the oil and gas market is expected to continue, offset by higher sales of data center products, which generally carry lower gross margins than oil and gas products."
"Consistent with those goals, the Company is actively pursuing several initiatives to enhance and expand manufacturing capacity to meet the increasing demand from data center markets."
