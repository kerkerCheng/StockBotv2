1) CPO: Optical engines are co-packaged alongside the ASIC. NPO: Optical engines are mounted on the board near the compute device.

2) CPO presents high packaging complexity and serviceability challenges, since optics are integrated within the ASIC package. NPO uses established PCB assembly and packaging processes and supports system-level serviceability.

3) NPO is being evaluated as a practical architectural option that balances efficiency gains with manufacturing and operational considerations; an extension of CPO solutions, but simpler in implementation.

Boundary: Credo is an industry participant (SerDes/AEC supplier), not a neutral benchmark. No market share, adoption rate, or win-probability data.
