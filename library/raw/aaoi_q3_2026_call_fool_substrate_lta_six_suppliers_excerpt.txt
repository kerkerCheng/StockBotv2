【分析師 Timothy Savageaux（Northland）問：那份基板協議（他說的量是每月約 1.5 萬片）夠不夠、何時開始買】
"You announced an agreement for substrates. I think it was earlier this week, could have been a month ago, the way things are going here, but -- and my question was, it's a pretty substantial quantity. I think it's like 15,000 wafers a month. And is that enough to support your target kind of run rate level exiting '27 of up near $500 million a month?"
"And then when that agreement kind of goes into effect when you expect to start purchasing those substrates?"

【執行長 Thompson Lin（轉錄稿標「Chih-Hsiang Lin」）答：協議主要是為 2028 年，因為供應商還在擴產】
"Yes, it'll be much, much bigger. And this -- actually, the agreement is more for, like, for 2028 because the supplier's still expanding the capacity."
"For the newest MOCVD, G10 MOCVD, every month, every MOCVD you need about 600 4-inch substrates."

【同一段：基板有六家供應商；簽長約是為了確保供給與存貨；同時和日本兩家、德國一家合作】
"Right now, as we emphasize, we have six suppliers for substrate, but we are working with all of them. But to make sure we have enough supply, including enough inventory because of the potential risk factor. That's why we signed an LTA with this supplier. And actually, we are working with other suppliers at the same time too, including two in Japan and one in Germany."
