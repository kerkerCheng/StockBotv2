NOTE: 西方衛星平台商（太空太陽電池的買方；它最大的客戶是美國太空發展署 SDA，見它 10-Q）自己的公告。用途：太空三結電池片層「國外那一半」第一份買方一手——它寫三五族（III-V，三結砷化鎵屬之）太空太陽電池「capacity constrained」、交期超過兩年；同一份公告的對策是併購矽太空電池廠 Solestial（自己整合進衛星）。兩個方向同時在一份文件裡：①買方寫交期與產能受限（讀圖反證 #2 推向量〔國外〕、敘事加碼條件 c1 的字面）；②買方往矽太空電池走（讀圖反證 #3／敘事反證第 4 條「改用矽」的方向；字面是併購與整合，沒有寫哪一個計畫已經改用）。沒有點名 AZUR／5N Plus。買方在推銷自己剛買的替代品，講三五族的缺點有立場——但交期與產能是它當買方的經驗，L8 下算買方一手。

【買方寫三五族電池的交期與產能】
"As demand for power in space accelerates, the industry faces a growing gap between mission requirements and available, scalable solutions."
"Legacy III-V solar solutions remain expensive and capacity constrained, with lead times exceeding two years, while terrestrial silicon alternatives are not designed for space and degrade rapidly under radiation exposure."
"Both approaches also depend heavily on Chinese-controlled materials and manufacturing, creating supply chain risk for critical missions."

【買方的對策：併購矽太空電池廠、收進自己的供應鏈】
"The acquisition secures a critical space solar capability within York’s supply chain through U.S.-based manufacturing, strengthening control over a key subsystem while reducing reliance on Chinese-controlled materials and manufacturing."
"Headquartered in Tempe, Arizona, Solestial has developed a differentiated approach to space solar, producing ultrathin, radiation-hardened silicon solar cells and modules engineered specifically for the space environment."
"Upon closing, Solestial will operate as a wholly owned subsidiary of York, continuing to serve customers across commercial, civil, and national security markets."
