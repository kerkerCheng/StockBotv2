"As previously disclosed, on June 24, 2026, Churchill Capital Corp XI, a Cayman Islands exempted company ("Churchill"), entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger … by and among Churchill, BLB Merger Sub, Inc., … and Agility Robotics, Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Agility")."
"As previously disclosed, Churchill has scheduled an extraordinary general meeting of its shareholders (the "Extraordinary General Meeting") for November 2, 2026, at 9:00 a.m. Eastern Time, to consider and vote upon, among other matters, a proposal to approve the Merger Agreement and the proposed transaction"
"Item 7.01. Regulation FD Disclosure. On October 30, 2026, Churchill and Agility jointly issued a press release announcing, among other things, that following the expiration of Churchill's redemption deadline, Agility's gross capital raise in connection with the proposed transaction and related financings is expected to be approximately $465 million, before transaction expenses."
"Shareholders who wish to withdraw their previously submitted redemption request may do so at any time prior to the scheduled vote at the Extraordinary General Meeting."
