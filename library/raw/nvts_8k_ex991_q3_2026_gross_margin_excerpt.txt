Third Quarter 2026 Financial Highlights：
"Gross Margin: GAAP gross margin for the quarter was 21.5%, compared to 0.4% in the second quarter of 2026 and (2.0%) in the third quarter of 2025."
"GAAP gross margin includes approximately $2.5 million of cost associated with amortization of intangibles for the current quarter and $4.0 million for each of the prior periods."
"On a non-GAAP basis, gross margin for the quarter was 40.0% compared to 39.5% in the prior quarter and 38.7% in the third quarter of 2025."

Fourth Quarter 2026 Business Outlook：
"Non-GAAP gross margin is expected to be 40.5%, plus or minus 100 basis points, which at the midpoint represents a 50-basis point increase."

Business, Customer and Technology Highlights：
"Announced first shipments of the latest Gen 5 GaNFast devices manufactured in the United States through GlobalFoundries to accelerate AI infrastructure deployments."
