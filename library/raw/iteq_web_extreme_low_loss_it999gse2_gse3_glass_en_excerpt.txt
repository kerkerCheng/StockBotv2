"High Tg (Extreme Low-Loss) IT-999GSE2 IT-999GSE2 The IT-999GSE2 is an advanced low CTE, high Tg ( 180°C by DMA), halogen free, low Dk and extreme-low loss material. This material is especially designed for leading edge high speed applications, like the 224Gbps per channel data rates using PAM-4 signaling. The product is designed for enterprise applications ranging from 1.6T Gbps data rates into multi-channel terabit rates. The product uses Low Dk2 glass which helps with the skew related issues. The prepreg constructions are designed with spread glass and laminates offer multi-ply spread glass constructions to reduce the fiber weave effect. IT-999GSE2 also exhibits high thermal and CAF reliability.
