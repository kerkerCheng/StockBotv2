【附註十七 部門說明（Automation Enabling Technologies 賣什麼）】
"The Automation Enabling Technologies segment designs, manufactures and markets laser beam steering and scanning solutions, laser sources, robotic and precision motion, robotic end-of-arm tooling, and bearing spindles to customers worldwide."

【MD&A — Automation Enabling Technologies（第三季）】
"Automation Enabling Technologies segment revenue for the three months ended October 2, 2026 increased $20.0 million, or 16.0%, versus the prior year period, primarily due to a 17.3% increase in sales of robotics and automation products and 13.7% increase in precision manufacturing products, associated with end market demand and new product introductions."

【Risk Factors（稀土出口許可）】
"For example, certain products within our Precision Manufacturing and Robotics and Automation businesses use rare-earth materials sourced from suppliers in China and magnets manufactured with such materials, the export of which requires a license from the Chinese government. For several months, we have sought but have been unable to obtain the licenses necessary to receive certain shipments of these materials, which has caused delays in fulfilling customer orders and increases in certain manufacturing costs."
