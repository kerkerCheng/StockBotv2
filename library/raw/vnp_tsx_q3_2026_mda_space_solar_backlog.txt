Specialty Semiconductors revenue reached $100.7 million in Q3 2026, an increase of 34% compared with Q3 2025. Revenue growth was driven primarily by higher volumes in terrestrial renewable energy and space solar power. The Company also began to see opportunities in recycling and refining complex germanium -bearing feeds. Adjusted EBITDA increased by 35% to $25.9 million, driven by higher volumes.
