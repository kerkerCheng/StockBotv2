[EXCERPT — 有限逐字引文] GlobalFoundries 新聞稿（MALTA, N.Y., Oct. 8, 2026）：與 TSMC 簽製造協議，在美國建立 CoWoS 用矽中介層的供應
RETRIEVED: 2026-10-18（gf.com 原始 HTML 去標籤後逐字核對；EDGAR 在 2026-09-08 至 10-14 之間沒有對應的 6-K）
NOTE: 原文沒有寫協議金額（WebFetch 摘要曾冒出「valued at $2 billion」，原始頁面沒有這句——不採）。

【第一段】
"GlobalFoundries (Nasdaq: GFS) (GF) today announced a manufacturing agreement with TSMC to establish a U.S.-based supply of silicon interposers for TSMC’s CoWoS ® advanced packaging ecosystem."

【第一段】
"Under the agreement, GF will provide manufacturing service to TSMC, and add fabrication capacity at its Malta, New York facility, supporting growing demand for high-performance computing and AI systems."

【第二段】
"GF expects to expand capacity at its Malta facility and establish the first U.S.-based source of silicon interposers supporting advanced packaging technologies, including embedded deep trench capacitor components."

【時程與年限】
"Volume production is expected to begin ramping at GF’s Malta site during the first half of 2028. The agreement has an initial term of five years and provides a framework for future capacity expansion."
