DA 26-786 — Released: July 28, 2026

We update the Covered List to include: ‘Foreign-produced advanced robotic devices, except advanced robotic devices which have been granted a Conditional Approval by DoW.’

Under the Commission’s existing rules in section 2.903(a), once added to the Covered List, ‘covered’ equipment is prohibited from receiving equipment authorizations.

The newly-covered equipment is identified by place of production, not by entity.
