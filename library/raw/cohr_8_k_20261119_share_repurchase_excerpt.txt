【Item 7.01 — Share Repurchase Program】
"On November 18, 2026, the Board of Directors of Coherent Corp. (the “Company”) authorized a share repurchase program under which the Company may repurchase up to $2.0 billion of its common stock, exclusive of any fees, commissions or other expenses related to such repurchases. The repurchase program authorizes the Company to purchase its common stock from time to time in the open market, through negotiated purchases including accelerated buyback programs, in block transactions with an institutional broker, pursuant to Rule 10b5-1 plans, through the sale of put options or other derivative transactions that may result in the Company’s acquisition of its capital stock, or otherwise, all in compliance with applicable laws, rules, regulations and other restrictions."
"The program has no expiration date, does not obligate the Company to repurchase any specific number of shares and may be suspended or discontinued at any time."
