[EXCERPT — 有限逐字引文] Wärtsilä 新聞稿（2026-06-29）：Wärtsilä secures new order to power next wave of AI-driven data center growth in the U.S.——Liberty Energy 下 2.92 億歐元燃氣引擎訂單（34SG），2029–2030 交到現場
RETRIEVED: 2026-10-27（本機原文抽字逐字核對）

【導言】
"Technology group Wärtsilä will supply engines for a power plant to ensure reliable supply of energy for large-scale data center development in the U.S."

【第 1 段】
"The €292 million order, expected to be booked by Wärtsilä in Q3 2026, was placed by Denver-based Liberty Energy, a leading U.S. energy services company."

【第 2 段】
"AI-driven data center projects require reliable, flexible on-site power capacity that can be rapidly deployed to offer predictable performance and availability."

【產品段】
"Wärtsilä 34SG engines run on natural gas, a platform proven to perform in demanding, high-temperature environments."

【末段】
"The equipment delivery to site is scheduled to begin in 2029 and continue through 2030."
