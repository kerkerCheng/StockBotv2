EX-99.1 2 q3fy27pr.htm EX-99.1 Document NVIDIA Announces Financial Results for Third Quarter Fiscal 2027 •Revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103% from a year ago •Data Center revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110% from a year ago •Vera Rubin NVL72, the most versatile and advanced computing platform ever built, has shipped SANTA CLARA, Calif.—Nov. 17, 2026―NVIDIA (NASDAQ: NVDA) today reported revenue for the third quarter ended October 25, 2026, of $115.8 billion, up 20% from the previous quarter and up 103% from a year ago. For the quarter, GAAP and non-GAAP gross margins were 74.8% and 74.9%, respectively. GAAP and non-GAAP earnings per diluted share were $2.70 and $2.68, respectively.

[EX-99.1｜anchor] Vera Rubin is off to an explosive start, our roadmap is on track
“Our momentum is real. NVIDIA is an exceptional technology leader, powered by the relentless innovation of our people,” said Colette Kress, interim CEO and executive vice president and chief financial officer of NVIDIA. “Vera Rubin is off to an explosive start, our roadmap is on track and our ambition has never been greater.” In September 2026, NVIDIA’s Board of Directors approved an increase of $150 billion to the company’s share repurchase authorization, without expiration. During the third quarter of fiscal 2027, NVIDIA returned $28.1 billion to shareholders in the form of shares repurchased and cash dividends. As of the end of the third quarter, the company had approximately $227.3 billion remaining under its share repurchase authorization.

[EX-99.2｜anchor] driven by the introduction of our Rubin infrastructure, our fastest architecture ramp ever
Data Center revenue for the third quarter was a record $107.6 billion, up 110% from a year ago and up 21% sequentially. Our Hyperscale and AI Clouds, Industrial, & Enterprise platforms both grew substantially, driven by the introduction of our Rubin infrastructure, our fastest architecture ramp ever, standalone Vera CPUs and the continued growth of Blackwell Ultra. Shipments of Data Center Hopper products to China during the quarter were $2.4 billion. Edge Computing revenue for the third quarter was $8.2 billion, up 42% from a year ago and up 14% sequentially. Strong Blackwell workstation sales and edge compute for Physical AI applications drove the increases, while higher memory and system prices continue to affect consumer PC demand.

[EX-99.2｜anchor] Our supply commitments increased from $279 billion last quarter to $390 billion
Commitments We have entered into strategic commitments across our supply, infrastructure, and partner ecosystems to capitalize on future growth opportunities and support our business. We’ve partnered with our extensive network of suppliers to secure the critical components needed to meet demand for the next several years. Our supply commitments increased from $279 billion last quarter to $390 billion, primarily related to the multi-year procurement of memory supply. Our cloud service agreements and data center lease commitments together of $102 billion, an increase from last quarter’s $74 billion, provide the physical and cloud infrastructure that powers our research and development — from the engineering, product design, and testing of our compute chips, networking products, and systems, to the development of our open models, such as NVIDIA Nemotron™, NVIDIA Cosmos™, and GR00T, and our autonomous vehicle software.
