[CEO prepared remarks — PhotonLink]
"It includes lasers, laser modules, complex fiber assemblies, and other optical components, and supports indium-phosphide-based CW lasers and VIXEL-based technologies."
"In Q1, we signed another significant PhotonLink LTA with a major AI company. The total revenue value of this new LTA is approximately $20 billion over five years, with revenue beginning to ramp in early calendar 28 . This is in addition to our previously announced LTA with NVIDIA."
"We expect revenue to exceed $100 million in our March quarter and to exceed $250 million per quarter before the end of calendar 27 . Expected shipments in the current quarter include a range of CPO products, including our ultra-high power 400-milliwatt CW laser, our fiber assembly solutions, and other products."
"PhotonLink is initially being deployed in CPO scale-out applications. We expect to begin shipping PhotonLink products for CPO and NPO scale-up applications in the second half of Calendar 27 ."
