【逐字稿：Ankit Agarwal（MD）——接單】
In the first half of FY '27, we secured orders worth INR32,000 crores. This is roughly 4x the orders we won in the whole of FY '26. The largest win this quarter is a new 5-year agreement with a potential value of $1.2 billion. It is with another global hyperscaler for AI-ready optical solutions.

【同段】
This builds on the $1.11 billion product award letter for optical connectivity that we announced last quarter from another hyperscaler.

【同段——市占與附加銷售】
our global ex -China OFC market share increased to 9% in H1 FY '27, up from 7% in first half of FY '26.

【逐字稿：CFO——營收】
This quarter, revenue reached INR2,529 crores, representing growth of 145% year-on-year basis and 32% quarter-on-quarter basis.

【同段——EBITDA】
Our operational EBITDA for Q2 FY '27 came in at INR685 crores, delivering 27.1% margin or significant quarter-on-quarter expansion of 624 basis points.

【同段——一次性收益】
Note that reported EBITDA for Q2 FY '27 was INR846 crores, which includes a onetime net gain of INR161 crores from a U.S. tariff refund.

【同段——淨利】
PAT for Q2 FY '27 reached a record INR517 crores, driven by strong revenue growth, margin expansion. For the first half, PAT reached INR714 crores, up from INR14 crores in H1 FY '26

【同段——分部與地區】
our Data Center segment saw a strong momentum, contributing 33% in H1 FY '27.

【同段】
North America shares stood at 61% in H1 FY '27

【同段——在手訂單】
Our open order book stands at record INR35,274 crores, up 4.6x from INR7,687 crores at the start of this financial year.

【同段】
Of this, about INR3,021 crores is scheduled for execution in Q3 FY '27 and the rest over Q4 FY '27 and beyond.

【同段——營收結構】
As our multiyear agreements with hyperscalers and telecom operators scale up, more of our revenue now comes from the scheduled backlog and spot business makes up a negligible share.

【同段——擴產】
Our disciplined capital allocation entails a mix of brownfield expansion of preform, fiber and cable capacity by 50% and a greenfield facility for preterm solutions with a total capex of INR1,000 crores per year from 2027 to 2029.

【同段——FY29 目標】
Accordingly, we are upgrading our FY '29 EBITDA margin target to about 30%, up from 27% earlier, while maintaining our FY '29 revenue target at INR20,000 crores.
