=== Exhibit 99.1 ===
"EnerSys To Acquire Ultralife, Expanding Differentiated Battery Technologies and Accelerating Growth in Defense Market"
"EnerSys (NYSE: ENS), a global leader in stored energy solutions for industrial, infrastructure, and defense applications, today announced that it has entered into a definitive agreement to acquire Ultralife (NASDAQ:ULBI) for $16.00 per share in cash, representing a 166% premium to Ultralife’s unaffected closing share price on October 26, 2026. The transaction represents an enterprise value of approximately $320 million."
"Ultralife is a leading provider of rechargeable and non-rechargeable batteries, battery systems, and related accessories for military communications and other specialized applications. The company serves government, defense, and commercial customers globally and generated $191 million in revenue in fiscal 2025."
