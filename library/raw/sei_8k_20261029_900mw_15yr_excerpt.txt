【8-K Item 8.01】
"On October 29, 2026, a subsidiary of Solaris Energy Infrastructure, Inc. (the “Company”) entered into an agreement to provide approximately 900 megawatts of power generation, associated balance of plant equipment and operations and maintenance services to a leading, investment-grade, diversified industrial and technology company that is an existing customer of the Company."
"The agreement is for an initial term of 15 years for each deployment, with an option to extend for an additional 15 years. Deployments under the agreement are expected to begin in the first half of 2027 and scale through 2028."

【10-Q 附註（NovaLT16 燃機交貨權）】
"At closing, the Company paid Colusa $66.9 million as consideration for the assignment of its contractual delivery rights and paid Baker Hughes $64.3 million in satisfaction of overdue milestone invoices and suspension-related costs that had accrued prior to the assignment, for aggregate closing payments of $131.2 million recorded as construction in progress."
