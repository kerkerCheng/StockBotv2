【Item 1 General Business Overview】
"Power Solutions International, Inc., incorporated under the laws of the state of Delaware in 2011, designs, engineers, manufactures, markets and sells a broad range of advanced, emission-certified engines and power systems that are powered by a wide variety of clean, alternative fuels, including natural gas, propane, and biofuels, as well as gasoline and diesel options, within the power systems, industrial and transportation end markets."

【Item 1 終端市場表】
"Power Systems Electric Power Generation (“Gensets”) Large Custom Genset Enclosures Mobile and Stationary Gensets for: Emergency Standby Rental Prime Power Demand Response Microgrid Oil & Gas Data Center"

【Item 1 產品段】
"The Company sells engines and power systems, including both emission-certified compression ignition and spark-ignition internal combustion engines which are enabled by advanced controls to run on a wide variety of clean, alternative fuels, including natural gas, propane, and biofuels, as well as gasoline and diesel options, within the power systems, industrial and transportation end markets."

【Item 1 客戶段】
