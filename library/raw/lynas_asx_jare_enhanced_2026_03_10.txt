JARE will provide a firm commitment to purchase 5,000 tonnes NdPr per annum for Japanese industry with an agreed market-linked floor price... 75% of all HRE oxides produced by Lynas will be made available to Japanese industry, with a firm commitment from JARE to purchase the equivalent of 50% of all HRE oxides produced by Lynas... price floor of US$110/kg NdPr... 30% of the upside above US$150/kg NdPr, capped at a maximum of US$10 million each calendar year... JARE is a special purpose company established by JOGMEC and Sojitz Corporation. Lynas and JARE are parties to a long term senior loan facility. (Lynas ASX, 2026-03-10)
