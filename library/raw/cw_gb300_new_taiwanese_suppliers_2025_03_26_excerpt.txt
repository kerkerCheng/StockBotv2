【Change #2 段】
Change #2: More Couplers and Cold Plates Addressing heat dissipation is a key challenge for the GB300. Starting with the GB200, Nvidia fully adopted liquid cooling systems. Critical components include cold plates—filled with coolant and in direct contact with chips—and couplers that link the cold plates to the cooling infrastructure. While the GB200 used a single large cold plate to cover two GPUs and one CPU, the GB300’s removable GPU design has led to a new configuration: each GPU and CPU now has its own cold plate to improve cooling efficiency. As a result, the number of cold plates and couplers used in the GB300 will increase. Each GB200 compute tray used two cold plates and six couplers; GB300 trays will require six cold plates and 14 couplers. Morgan Stanley notes that established thermal management firms Asia Vital Components (AVC) and Cooler Master will remain key suppliers of cold plates for the GB300 platform. Cooler Master, Fositek, FIT, and Lotes are expected to compete for coupler contracts.
