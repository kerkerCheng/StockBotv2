【風險因子：包銷買方 SPV 的資金】
"US SIIE is a special purpose vehicle capitalized by the U.S. government and private capital sources, and if the U.S. government or private capital sources fail to provide adequate capitalization to US SIIE, including as a result of changes in administration, policy priorities or appropriations, US SIIE may lack the financial resources necessary to perform its purchase obligations under the Offtake Agreement."

【風險因子：SPV 優先債尚未簽署】
"The Senior Debt Facility to be made available to US SIIE has not been documented, closed or funded and remains subject to numerous conditions."

【風險因子：銀行承諾的期限】
"In addition, the commitment terminates at the earliest of (i) 11:59 p.m. (New York time) on December 31, 2026 and (ii) the suspension, termination or cancellation of the Offtake Agreement, in each case unless the closing of the Senior Debt Facility has occurred on or before such date, and US SIIE may terminate the commitment at any time, with or without cause."

【Note DFC 融資協議：額度與餘額】
"providing for a senior secured term loan facility in an aggregate principal amount not to exceed $ 565.0 million (the “Facility”), of which $ 325.0 million (the “U.S. DFC Loan Agreement”) remained outstanding at the SV Acquisition Date and remains outstanding at September 30, 2026. At the SV Acquisition Date, the Company assumed the outstanding loan balance at a fair value of $ 305.5 million."

【Note DFC 融資協議：利率】
"Annual interest accrues at a floating rate based on Term Secured Overnight Financing Rate (“SOFR”) plus a 4.0 % spread"

【Note DFC 融資協議：期限與攤還】
"The term of the Facility is 15 years from the initial disbursement date. The Facility is repayable in up to forty-eight ( 48 ) quarterly sculpted installments commencing 37 months following the initial disbursement date."

【風險因子摘要：磁材確定合約】
"We may not be able to convert current commercial discussions and/or memorandums of understanding with customers for the sale of our products into definitive contracts and we may not procure or maintain profitable purchase orders for our products, which may have a negative effect on our business."

【風險因子摘要：中國出口管制名單】
"Being designated on an export control list by China has had and is expected to continue to have an adverse impact on our ability to source key raw materials and supplies from China, which has impacted and is expected to continue to impact our business."

"Plaintiffs seek a temporary and permanent injunction against further alleged use and possession of their allegedly protected technology, unspecified monetary damages, and attorneys’ fees. The Company disputes the allegations and intends to vigorously defend against such claims."
