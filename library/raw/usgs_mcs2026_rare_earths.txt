USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries, February 2026 — RARE EARTHS. Bastnaesite was mined as a primary product in Mountain Pass, CA. Import Sources (2021-24): Rare-earth compounds and metals: China, 71%; Malaysia, 13%; Japan, 5%; Estonia, 5%; and other, 6%. Substitutes are available for many applications but generally are less effective. Net import reliance as a percentage of apparent consumption, compounds and metals: 67 (2025e).
