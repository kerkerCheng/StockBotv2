"TAIPEI, Taiwan — May 28, 2026 — Wiwynn, an innovative cloud IT infrastructure provider for data center, today announced it will unveil its latest Co-Packaged Optics (CPO) interconnect technologies at Computex 2026 (Nangang Exhibition Center Hall 1, Booth #J0106). The demonstration brings together ecosystem partners including Ayar Labs, Global Unichip Corp. (GUC), Browave, Corning Incorporated, FOCI, Molex, SENKO, and TE Connectivity. Together, the companies will present a full-stack path from chip-level CPO innovation to data center-scale deployment, marking a pivotal step forward for AI scale-up architectures."

"To accelerate adoption, Wiwynn has formed a strategic partnership with CPO leader Ayar Labs. By integrating Ayar Labs' industry-leading TeraPHY™ optical engines and External Laser Small Form Factor Pluggable (ELSFP) SuperNova™ light sources into Wiwynn's rack-level architecture, the two companies are jointly delivering high-bandwidth, power-efficient CPO connectivity. Together, they are addressing key hyperscaler deployment challenges: fiber management, CPO-enabled AI ASIC integration, thermal management, power efficiency, and manufacturability."
