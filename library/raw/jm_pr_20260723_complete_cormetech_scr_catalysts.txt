【導言】
"Johnson Matthey Plc (“JM”) has today announced the completion of its acquisition of CORMETECH Inc."

【About Cormetech】
"Founded in 1989, Cormetech is a globally leading, high-growth US manufacturer of Selective Catalytic Reduction (“SCR”) catalysts serving over 400 long-standing customers."

【策略理由】
"In particular, Cormetech's IP in gas turbines and module designs will enable JM to offer customised best-in-class catalysts and systems for a range of energy platforms."

【策略理由】
"It is expected to materially enhance Clean Air Solutions’ scale, helping to establish a combined deep market presence in the US’s power generation market, which is seeing strong demand through the rapid construction of data centres."
