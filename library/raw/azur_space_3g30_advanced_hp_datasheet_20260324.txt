【規格書 p.1 首段】
AZUR SPACE’s 3G30-Advanced bare solar cells and solar cell assemblies (“SCA”), or CICs (Cell-interconnect-coverglass) are the ideal solution for space power applications in conventional or in New Space segments.

【規格書 p.1 第二段（3G30C-Advanced 裸片）】
The 3G30C-Advanced bare cell device is a space-qualified InGaP/GaAs/Ge triple-junction solar cell originating from a 150 mm germanium (Ge) substrate and provides 30% class beginning-of-life (BOL) efficiency.

【規格書 p.1 第三段（3G30A 組件）】
The 3G30A assembly includes a bare 3G30C-Advanced solar cell, a corner-mounted silicon bypass diode, welded in-plane silver-coated Kovar interconnectors (“ICs”), and a 100 µm AR coated coverglass bonded with space-grade heritage adhesive.

【規格書 p.1 第五段（鑑定與產地）】
The 3G30-Advanced technology, cells and assemblies are ECSS-E-ST-20-08C (Rev. 1) qualified. These devices are all produced in our mature MRL-10 Heilbronn facility.
