Sojitz has begun the import of heavy rare earths (HREs) produced by Australia-based Lynas Rare Earths Ltd into Japan... In March 2023, Sojitz entered into an agreement with Lynas to secure a supply of dysprosium and terbium... Sojitz has previously provided financing to Lynas through loan and equity investment... first import of rare earth products made using ore sourced from Australia that is separated and refined in Malaysia.
