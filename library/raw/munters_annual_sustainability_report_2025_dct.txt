【p.8 The year in brief（Record year for DCT）】
In 2025, Munters business area DCT delivered a record year, driven by strong demand and focused work in close partnership with customers.

【p.37 Data Center Technologies（事業定位）】
Data Center Tecnologies (DCT) is a technology-leading provider of advanced cooling systems for data centers.

【p.37 DCT Key events during 2025】
Implemented the LCX platform (CDU) with key customers to enable scalable liquid cooling

【p.39 Two loops that together enable efficient data center cooling】
Coolant distribution units (CDU) and liquid cooling evaporators (LCE) serve as the interface between the two loops.

【p.41 DCT Market development and financial performance 2025 — Order intake】
Organic order intake increased by +247%, driven by strong demand for CDUs, CRAHs, and chillers. Growth was particularly strong in the Americas, with orders from both colocators and hyperscalers, reflecting a strengthened market position and an expanded offering during the year, supported by continued strong underlying demand.

【p.66 Board of Directors' report — significant events（12 月紀錄訂單）】
In December, DCT received new record orders with a total value of approx. BSEK 2.1 from a US-based colocation data center customer. The orders comprise chilled water Computer Room Air Handlers (CRAHs), Coolant Distribution Units (CDUs), Geoclima Circlemiser chillers, and related start-up and com- missioning services for large-scale data center cool- ing infrastructure. Deliveries are scheduled to begin in Q4 2026 and continue through Q1 2028.
