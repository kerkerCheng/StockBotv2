（§2.3 Impact、§2.4 Scale）2.3 Impact The specification will enable any CDU supplier in the industry to develop, manufacture and improve upon the “Project Deschutes” design which is informed by more than 7 years of scale liquid cooling experience at Google. Furthermore,1st party hyperscalers and 3rd party data center owners alike all over the world would be able purchase a CDU based on the “Project Deschutes” specification. 2.4 Scale The “Project Deschutes” CDU is assembled from components that are sourced from multiple vendors that are widely known in the industry. Custom components in the CDU are intended to be manufactureable by well-known processes. Beyond supply chain considerations, installation and maintenance procedures are shared to enable fast deployments of reliable equipment. The processes and best practices shared by Google are based on the 7+ years of scale liquid cooling experience.

（§3.2 Product requirements）3.2 Product requirements 1. Weight (wet, max): 6900 lbs 2. Weight (dry, max): 5310 lbs 3. Maximum height with packaging: 100 inches 4. N+1 (pump and accessories) 5. Pump serviceability (front & rear, front preferred) 6. Filtration, front serviceable (44 micron) 7. Side stream (i.e. bypass/parallel) filtration, front serviceable (0.2 micron), ~3 GPM 8. Spirovent (air separator) 9. Flow-through expansion tank, front serviceable 10. Leak rope detection (detected through PLC, 3 zones) 11. Telemetry (temperature & pressure on both sides, CDU flow, pump speed, pump power) 12. Flow meter (+/- 0.5% of reading) 13. Wetted materials: stainless steel, copper, EPDM 14. Fill and purge ports
