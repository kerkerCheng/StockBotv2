【MD&A｜營收】
"Revenue increased $45.5 million, or 162.6%, to $73.4 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 from $28.0 million for the three months ended September 30, 2025. The substrate revenue increase for the three months ended September 30, 2026 as compared to the same period in 2025 was primarily the result of higher demand for InP wafer substrates used for data center applications and in passive optical networks and GaAs wafer substrates, as a result of additional export approvals granted by the China government."

China 40,284／13,145（+206.5%，占 55%）；Taiwan 7,367／9,045（−18.6%）；Japan 535／1,854（−71.1%）；Asia Pacific (excluding China, Taiwan and Japan) 9,722／347；Europe 14,823／3,424（+332.9%）；North America 679／140；Total 73,410／27,955。
"Revenue in Taiwan decreased $1.7 million, primarily due to the impact of China export restrictions on our InP wafer substrates and lower demand for our GaAs wafer substrates. Revenue in Japan decreased $1.3 million, primarily due to the impact of China export restrictions on our InP wafer substrates and lower demand for our GaAs wafer substrates."
"Revenue in Europe increased $11.4 million, primarily due to additional export approvals granted by the China government for our InP and GaAs wafer substrates. Revenue in North America increased $0.5 million, primarily due to additional export approvals granted by the China government for our InP wafer substrates."

【通美｜PE 贖回】
"Although each Investor had the right to require the redemption of its shares at the original value, no Investor elected to be redeemed. To show good faith to the Investors, Tongmei allowed each Investor to retain its shares in exchange for surrendering any future right of redemption. Twelve of the thirteen Investors agreed. One Investor's internal rules required an exit, resulting in the transfer of some of its shares to a third party with the balance repurchased by Tongmei, subsequent to September 30, 2026."
