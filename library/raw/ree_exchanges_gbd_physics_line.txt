Excerpt:
Grain boundary diffusion is especially effective for relatively thin magnets used in electric vehicles but becomes increasingly challenging for thicker magnets used in large wind turbines and certain defense systems. Grain boundary diffusion reduces heavy rare earth intensity. It does not eliminate the need for dysprosium, terbium, or advanced magnet manufacturing expertise.
