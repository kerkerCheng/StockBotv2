Excerpt:
Since Washington tightened controls on exports of certain advanced HBM products to China in December 2024, Chinese chipmakers have increasingly relied on grey-market channels to secure supplies
Such HBM typically costs several times what buyers outside China pay
The price hikes illustrate how a worldwide shortage of HBM is reverberating through China's AI industry
Because memory accounts for a large share of an AI accelerator's production cost, the higher prices are feeding directly into finished cards
The advanced HBM market is dominated by South Korea's SK Hynix and Samsung Electronics, and US-based Micron Technology
