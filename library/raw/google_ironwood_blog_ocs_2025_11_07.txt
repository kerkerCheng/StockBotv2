"Scaling with OCS: Pods and Superpods To scale beyond a single cube, multiple cubes are connected using an Optical Circuit Switch (OCS) network. This is a dynamic, reconfigurable optical network that connects entire cubes, allowing the system to scale from a small "pod" (e.g., a 256-chip Ironwood pod with four cubes) to a massive "superpod" (e.g., a 9,216-chip system with 144 cubes). This OCS-based topology is key to fault tolerance. If a cube or link fails, the OCS fabric manager instructs the OCS to optically bypass the unhealthy unit and establish new, complete optical circuits connecting only the healthy cubes, swapping in a designated spare."
