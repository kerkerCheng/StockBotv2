NOTE: CPO 全堆疊測試層（tech:cpo_full_stack_test）與 FORM 敘事兩條反證的對照：TRITON（CPO 光學探測系統）2026 年營收預期由 1,000–2,000 萬美元上修到「north of $30 million」；Systems 分部第三季 5,950 萬美元創高、季增 23%（TRITON 占比沒拆）；CEO 說 CPO 這一側「still really dominated by 1 ecosystem」、開始擴到 NPO 與可插拔；沒有講 TRITON 交期拉長、配額或客戶預付；沒有上修 2030 年可服務市場。Teradyne 10 月投資 3 億美元（測試設備夥伴入股，不是客戶預付）。全公司第三季營收 2.805 億美元（+38.4%）、2026 全年將首度超過 10 億美元；Farmers Branch 新廠 2027 年底可支撐約 4 億美元探針卡年營收。

Mike Slessor (CEO): "Turning now to our Systems segment. Systems delivered a record quarter, growing 20% sequentially on accelerating demand for our TRITON system for co-packaged optics or CPO, applications. This accelerating demand has been evident in the steady increase in our expectations for this business in 2026. We started the year expecting between $10 million and $20 million of TRITON revenue and now expect upwards of $30 million in 2026."
"In tandem with this growing CPO business, we're also serving demand for additional photonics applications such as CPO's close cousin near package optics or NPO as well as pluggable devices. … In this case, we've prioritized building leadership in Test Insertion One, which ensures known good die or KGD, of the photonic integrated circuit or PIC."
"Both CPO and NPO architectures require KGD PICs before they are combined with the electronic ICs to form the optical module and scale up and scale out network switches. As a result, FormFactor benefits from broader photonics adoption, whether it be CPO or NPO."
Q&A (Craig Ellis asked about TRITON durability): "we've accelerated our expectations now to north of $30 million in TRITON revenue for 2026 after starting the year at between $10 million and $20 million Customers continue to be very bullish. It's still really dominated by 1 ecosystem, at least on the CPO side. But as I mentioned in the prepared remarks, one of the other things we're seeing is some diversification where we're seeing adoption in NPO applications and some pluggables applications as well. In pluggables, not necessarily the TRITON system, but a derivative technology."
"Specifically, during the third quarter, we shipped probe cards to the world's leading foundry for 1 single GPU design in multimillion-dollar volumes."
"The midpoint of our fourth quarter outlook puts us on track to exceed $1 billion of revenue in 2026"
Aric McKinnis (CFO): "Q3 revenues of $280.5 million came in $10.5 million above the midpoint of our Q3 outlook range … Systems segment revenues grew on higher TRITON Photonics system revenues, reaching a new record of $59.5 million in Q3, up $11 million or 23%, from Q2."
"System gross margin increased by more than 10 percentage points to 61% about half of the system gross margin improvement from Q2 was driven by record revenue volumes that created significant operating leverage on fixed costs, primarily due to increased sales of our TRITON Photonics systems."
"we announced a collaboration with Teradyne. This included a $300 million investment in FormFactor in order to formalize our partnership and reinforce the open ecosystem for probe card and ATE solutions."
"Exiting 2027, we expect Farmers Branch will have the capability to produce about $400 million in annual probe card revenue"
"We expect revenue of approximately $285 million, plus or minus $10 million" (Q4 outlook)
Takeaways (Motley Fool summary): "Non-GAAP Gross Margin -- 56.5%"; "Total Cash and Investments -- $399 million, an increase of $50 million during the third quarter before the $300 million Teradyne investment."; "HBM Outlook -- Management expects continued growth in HBM4E and HBM5 designs despite a temporary sequential decline in revenue during the third quarter."
