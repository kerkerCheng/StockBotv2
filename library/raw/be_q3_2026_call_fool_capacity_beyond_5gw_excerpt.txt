【問（JPMorgan Mark Strouse）】
"I was curious if you can just talk about recent decision to expand your real estate footprint in Fremont."
"And since you have talked about your current manufacturing footprint supporting over 5 gigs of annual output. Should we now assume that is over 10 gigs once that facility is up and running?"

【答（K. R. Sridhar，執行長）】
"But from a timing perspective, it is very obvious to us that the 5-gigawatt milestone which was just a milestone, in a long journey, we can clearly see that coming, and we need to get prepared for what is after that."
"And we will never increase our capacity in a step function."
"We were not going to provide a timeline of when that is gonna happen."
"But we have strong conviction and there is enough confidence we are getting from the marketplace, to tell us that we should start preparing for past the milestone of the 5-gigawatts."

【財務長 Simon Edwards（準備稿）】
"Capital expenditures $153 million in the quarter, and $231 million year to date. Driven primarily by investments in machinery and production capacity to support expansion within our existing Fremont and Newark facilities."
