【標題】
"MTAR reports another strong quarter, achieving its highest-ever quarterly revenue in Q2 FY27, with 200% YoY growth in revenue and 483.3% YoY growth in EBITDA"

【YoY Q2 FY27 vs Q2 FY26】
"Revenue from Operations stood at Rs. 406.8 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as against Rs. 135.6 Cr. in Q2 FY 26, 200.0% increase YoY"
"EBITDA reported at Rs. 99.2 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as compared to Rs. 17.0 Cr. in Q2 FY 26, 483.3% increase YoY"
"Profit After Tax was at Rs. 59.8 Cr in Q2 FY 27 as against Rs. 4.2 Cr. in Q2 FY 26, 1309.5% increase YoY"

【QoQ Q2 FY27 vs Q1 FY27】
"Revenue from Operations stood at Rs. 406.8 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as against Rs. 360.7 Cr. in Q1 FY 27, 12.8% increase QoQ"
"EBITDA reported at Rs. 99.2 Cr. in Q2 FY 27 as compared to Rs. 85.1 Cr. in Q1 FY 27 , 16.6% increase QoQ"

【執行長】
"We expect H2 FY27 to be stronger than H1, supported by a robust order book and sustained execution momentum, in line with our annual guidance."
