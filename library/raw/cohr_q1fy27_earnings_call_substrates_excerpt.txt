[17:21 James Anderson, Chief Executive Officer — prepared remarks]
"Expanding our indium phosphide capacity is a key enabler of growth in both indium phosphide pluggable transceivers and indium phosphide-enabled PhotonLink solutions. Our 6-inch indium phosphide expansion is progressing well. We doubled our internal indium phosphide output capacity year-over-year in Q1, one quarter ahead of our original plan. Q1 was our highest production quarter ever for EMLs, including both 100-gig and 200-gig devices. The ramp contributed meaningfully to data center growth, and we expect it to remain an important driver of revenue and gross margin. Most of the gross margin benefit of our indium phosphide expansion is still ahead of us, as the expansion will enable us to increase our mix of internally sourced indium phosphide components, which have a tremendous cost advantage. We remain on track to more than double our internal indium phosphide output capacity again by the end of calendar 27 . We've secured the substrates and other critical inputs needed to support this ramp, and given the strength of customer demand, are planning additional capacity expansion beyond 2027. Our 6-inch lines in Texas and Sweden are producing EMLs, CW lasers, and high-speed photodiodes, with yields that continue to exceed those of our 3-inch lines. Production includes our 400-milliwatt CW laser for CPO, which is covered by our Envidu partnership, and is expected to begin generating revenue this quarter. We also remain on track to begin 6-inch Indian phosphide production in Zurich in the first half of calendar 27 , further extending what we believe is a meaningful manufacturing advantage."

[1:12:37 Operator]
"The next question comes from Mita Marshall with Morgan Stanley. Please go ahead."

[1:12:43 Unidentified Analyst, Morgan Stanley]
"Great, thanks. Maybe just a question on substrate availability. I think there had been some concern in the market around availability of 6-inch versus 3-inch. I know you noted in the prepared remarks that you had gotten capacity, but maybe if you could just speak about kind of a six-inch versus three-inch substrate availability and how that's impacting expansion plans. Thanks."

[1:13:11 James Anderson, Chief Executive Officer]
"Yeah, thanks, Mita. So we have over on six-inch in particular, but three-inch as well. We do continue to buy three-inch wafers. We do still have three-inch production, but all of our expansion is mostly on six-inch moving forward. And we have over five different suppliers of substrates. We've signed long-term agreements with a number of those suppliers. And when I look at our next step of more than doubling the capacity by the end of calendar 27 , We believe we've secured the necessary substrates for that capacity expansion. And then we've certainly, you know, we have plans in place to further expand capacity beyond 27 , and we're certainly working with or already have in place substrate capacity for that as well. So I think the, you know, the summary is we have multiple different suppliers, and we've made sure that we have the substrate capacity for the expansion."
