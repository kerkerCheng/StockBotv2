【OCP Design Specification〈Universal Quick Disconnect (UQD) v2.0.0〉（Cooling Environments: Cold Plate Sub-Project；封面「Effective September 22, 2026」；opencompute.org 檔案 HTTP Last-Modified 2026-10-02；第三方規格・定稿；pypdf 文字層，=== p.N === 是頁碼標記）：§10 供應商交叉驗證】
"10. Supplier Cross-Verification Testing Suppliers shall cross-validate their parts with a minimum of 3 other suppliers. In accordance with the OCP tenet of Openness, UQD suppliers shall make their UQDs available to other UQD suppliers upon request for cross -verification purposes. Likewise, the requesting supplier shall make available their UQDs at the time of request. UQD Suppliers shall publish the supplier list with which their QDs have successfully passed cross-verification testing. The cross-verification test reports shall be made available to customers upon request. The required cross -verification tests, shown in Figure 15, are a subset of the QD Property Tests. These tests are considered critical to ensuring the interoperation of one supplier’s QDs with those of a different supplier. The tests may be run in parallel or series, and apply to both the plug and socket of each supplier . In accordance with ISO 18869, the required sample size per test is 3, at minimum. In accordance with ISO 18869:2017 Section 6.3, the instruments must meet certain accuracy requirements. Figure 15 - Cross-Verification Tests Cross-verification across previous versions of UQDs is not in scope of this specification . Performance, feature or dimensional variations across versions should be evaluated by the end user when considering mixed version deployments."
