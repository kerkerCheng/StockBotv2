NOTE: **第三方轉錄、公司自述（L8）**。講者：Casey Crenshaw（Executive Chairman 兼 Interim President & CEO）、Andrew Puhala（CFO）。Stabilis 敘事用：
①第二份資料中心合約（短期試運轉）8 月開始送氣、第四季結束——第二季法說說「could well extend」，這一季沒延長；
②長約 2027 年第一季中開始（先動員設備與測試）、第二三季爬坡、第四季才到滿載——2027 年認列的不是一整年；
③CFO：不給 2027 年指引（第二季法說說 2027 年營收超過 1 億美元）；
④資料中心增量主要用外購 LNG、正在敲定長約的供氣合約與備援氣源；
⑤非受限現金 150 萬美元；
⑥Galveston：已承諾承購約 25%（第三季 10-Q 寫約 16%——兩個數不一致，以 10-Q 為準，差異沒有解釋）、FID 不給時程；
⑦過渡電力合約的長度取決於電網併網時程，可能延長、「we just don't know yet」；長期備援是上一季最活躍的一類。

【Crenshaw 開場：短期試運轉合約】
"We began delivering LNG under the agreement in August."
"This project will conclude in the fourth quarter."
"While fourth quarter revenues will reflect the conclusion of our short-term data center project, we expect meaningful revenue growth in 2027."

【Crenshaw 開場：長約與供氣】
"Our multiyear agreement to supply LNG for behind-the-meter bridge power at a U.S. data center expected to be the largest contract to date in our company's history remains on track to begin in the first quarter of 2027 and will ramp during the second and third quarters."
"We are progressing as planned on the pre-commissioning for the project with more than $15 million of specific project equipment on order and scheduled for delivery ahead of start-up."
"We are also finalizing LNG supply agreements for the project, along with backup supply alternatives to support reliable delivery."

【Crenshaw 開場：Galveston】
"That said, we are not ready to commit to a time line for FID, which remains subject to arranging a final financing structure and securing sufficient commercial offtake."

【Puhala：現金與指引】
"Of the $18.4 million, $16.8 million is restricted to support equipment purchases and other preparations for the 2027 data center contract and $1.5 million is unrestricted."
"Nothing here is new, and we're not providing 2027 guidance, but with the third quarter now behind us, they're worth keeping in mind."
"Second, we'll service much of the incremental power generation demand with third-party LNG, so it requires limited incremental capital and preserves our plant capacity to support continued growth with our aerospace and other existing customers."
"Taken together and in the context of a materially larger revenue base in 2027, this should translate into meaningful growth in adjusted EBITDA dollars and margin expansion into the high-teens as execution accelerates."
