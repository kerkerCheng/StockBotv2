Key Highlights：
"Cash, cash equivalents and restricted cash balance as of September 30, 2026 of $1.2 billion"
"Revenues for Q3’2026 of $3.0 million"
"Generated first ever revenue from the sale of neodymium-iron-boron (“NdFeB”) permanent magnets"

Third Quarter Business Updates（Stillwater 磁材）：
"In September 2026, the Company sold the first NdFeb magnets produced at its 600 tpa magnet manufacturing facility in Stillwater, OK. The initial sales comprised sintered NdFeB block magnets, sold to a high-performance materials manufacturing company."
"The Company has installed the first magnet finishing line at Stillwater, with a second finishing line expected to be installed by the end of 2026, which will broaden the Company’s product offering."

Third Quarter Business Updates（Serra Verde）：
"This Stage 1 expansion is now ramping up to 4,000 metric tonnes per annum (“tpa”) of total rare earth oxide (TREO), while Stage 2, which is targeting to add a further 2,400 tpa of capacity by the end of 2027, is expected to complete construction and commence commissioning in Q3 2027."

Recent Developments：
"In October 2026, the Company commenced the shipping and sales of heavy mixed rare earth carbonate (“MREC”) to US SIEE, LLC under a 15-year, 100% take-or-pay offtake agreement."

Key Operational Targets：
"By end of 2026: Finished magnet prototype sales to multiple customers from Stillwater"
"Q2 2027: Commission Line 1b at Stillwater, adding an additional 600 tpa of magnet manufacturing capacity for a total of 1,200 tpa"
"Q2 2027: Serra Verde Stage 1 ramp complete, 4,000 tpa TREO run rate"

Forward-looking Statements（風險列舉，節錄兩項）：
"our ability to convert current commercial discussions and/or memorandums of understanding with customers for the sale of our neo magnets and other products into definitive orders"
"our designation on an export control list by China which has had and is expected to continue to have an adverse impact on our ability to source key raw materials, equipment, supplies and technical services from China"
