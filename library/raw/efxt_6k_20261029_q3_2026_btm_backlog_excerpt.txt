【新聞稿：ES 在手與訂單】
"Record ES backlog of $2.1 billion as at September 30, 2026 provides strong visibility into future revenue generation and business activity levels."
"Bookings of $1.0 billion during Q3/26 compared to $339 million in Q3/25, $488 million in Q2/26 and a trailing eight quarter average of $448 million."
"ES book-to-bill ratio (calculated as bookings divided by revenue) was 3.0x during Q3/26 and 1.3x on a trailing eight-quarter average"

【MD&A：在手增加的原因】
"ES Backlog of $2.1 billion at September 30, 2026 increased from $1.1 billion at December 31, 2025 primarily due to a newly awarded BTM natural gas-fired power generation project of approximately 450 megawatts for a NAM data center developer, partially offset by advancement of ES projects in NAM during the nine months ended September 30, 2026."

【MD&A：季後超過 1 GW 的合約】
"Additional Power Generation Award: Subsequent to September 30, 2026, Enerflex secured an award to supply over 1.0 gigawatts of low emission BTM natural gas-fired power generation for a North American data center development."
"Combined with additional project awards, including the approximately 450 megawatts recorded in the third quarter, Enerflex’s backlog of BTM natural gas-fired power generation now exceeds 1.75 gigawatts, with deliveries extending through 2028."
"This additional 1.0 gigawatt award will be reflected in ES backlog during the fourth quarter of 2026."

【新聞稿：ES 毛利】
"ES gross margin before depreciation and amortization was 17% in Q3/26, compared to 17% in Q3/25 and 18% in Q2/26"

【MD&A：擴廠】
"As previously announced, Enerflex has authorized investments in its ES facilities and capabilities, including $30 million to $40 million included in Enerflex’s 2026 capital expenditure guidance and approximately $85 million expected to be incurred during 2027."
"These investments, combined with on-going initiatives to enhance collaboration, leverage scale, improve operational efficiency, and solidify capabilities, are expected to significantly increase revenue capacity of Enerflex’s ES business line."

【財務體質】
"Enerflex’s bank-adjusted net debt-to-EBITDA ratio was approximately 0.6x at the end of Q3/26, down from 1.2x at the end of Q3/25 and 0.8x at the end of Q2/26"
"At September 30, 2026, the Company was in compliance with its covenants."
