Title: AOI Introduces New Ultra High-Power Semiconductor Laser to Support Silicon Photonics and CPO
Published: 2025-12-18

Research excerpt:
- AAOI announced a 400-milliwatt narrow-linewidth pump laser for silicon photonics and CPO in AI data centers.
- The product is described as a high-performance light source for CPO and silicon photonics, with optical power over 400mW at 50°C.
- Samples were available to select customers; volume production later in 2026 was guidance.

Boundary: the official release does not name a customer, disclose a design win, or confirm commercial revenue. The Serenity lead's exact CEO 'TBD' wording was not independently obtained.
