① 聯亞 113 年度年報 致股東報告書：
「113 年度，隨著人工智能（AI）需求的持續擴大，數據中心相關產品持續為公司帶來營收的增長。」
「本年度營業收入為新台幣 12.08 億元，較前一年增長了 14.4%；稅後淨損為 0.54 億元，相較 2023 年度虧損幅度減少 74%；每股稅後虧損縮小至 0.59 元。」
「展望 114 年，AI 技術將持續在全球各行業中快速發展，隨著數據傳輸需求規格的升級，以及美國、大陸地區的數據中心持續增建，800G/1.6T 以上高速光收發模組的應用需求持續快速增加。而矽光技術也將躍為往後的主流解決方案，預期能為公司帶來強勁的營收成長動能。」

③ AXT, Inc.——"AXT Inc. Announces First Quarter 2025 Financial Results"（新聞稿，2025-05-01；https://investors.axt.com/Investors/news/news-details/2025/AXT-Inc--Announces-First-Quarter-2025-Financial-Results/default.aspx；HTML 轉文字、逐字照抄；RETRIEVED 2026-10-07）：
"Revenue for the first quarter of 2025 was $19.4 million, compared with $25.1 million for the fourth quarter of 2024 and $22.7 million for the first quarter of 2024."
"GAAP gross margin was (6.4) percent of revenue for the first quarter of 2025, compared with 17.6 percent of revenue for the fourth quarter of 2024 and 26.9 percent for the first quarter of 2024."
"The company’s decline in GAAP and non-GAAP gross margin from the prior quarter was primarily the result of three factors: a 58 percent reduction in indium phosphide sales quarter-over-quarter due to trade restrictions in China, a significant yield reduction on semi-insulating gallium arsenide wafers, and under-absorbed factory overhead due to lower substrate sales."
"Tongmei, our China subsidiary, is uniquely positioned to optimize growth opportunities in China, such as high-speed data center connectivity and LIDAR for autonomous driving, and are pursuing these and other opportunities with success across key markets for indium phosphide, gallium arsenide, and germanium substrates."
