Title: AXT Form 8-K — Coherent 6-inch InP development and supply agreement
Filed: 2026-07-02; earliest event: 2026-06-26

Research excerpt:
- AXT-Tongmei and Coherent entered a three-year mass-development and supply agreement for 6-inch InP wafer substrates.
- AXT committed to expand the relevant Beijing capacity in 2026-2028 for a US$22,288,500 Coherent prepayment.
- Unused prepayment can be refundable; Coherent can terminate if AXT misses the capacity commitment for more than six successive months.

Boundary: this is AXT issuer disclosure, not Coherent-side independent confirmation; it does not establish sole source, qualification, realized volume, or revenue recognition.
