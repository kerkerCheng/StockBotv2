INTRODUCTION

The Alfa Laval CB410 copper brazed plate heat exchanger (BHE) is engineered to provide efficient heat transfer with a compact footprint. The brazing material seals and holds the plates together at the contact points ensuring optimal heat transfer efficiency and pressure resistance. Using advanced design technologies and extensive verification guarantees the highest performance and longest possible service life.

Alfa Laval CB410AQ is AHRI Certified through the Liquid to Liquid Heat Exchangers (LLHE) Certification Program which ensures thermal performance in accordance with the product specifications.

POSITION IN DATA CENTER

The plate heat exchanger may be an option for an In-row & Centralised Cooling Distribution Unit (CCDU, large skid based CDUs) and functions as a circuit breaker between the medias in the system, minimizing the volume of the coolant required.

The CB410 may also be used in chillers as a free cooling heat exchanger and as a interface heat exchanger for waste heat recovery.
