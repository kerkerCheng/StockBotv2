[10-K FY2026｜anchor] Our advanced packaging portfolio including the APTURA
We recently introduced ASTERION™ TW, an ultrasonic terminal welding system designed to support next-generation power module manufacturing. ASTERION™ TW uses a solid-state bonding process and is intended for high-reliability power applications, including renewable energy, transportation and data center applications Our advanced packaging portfolio including the APTURA™ fluxless thermo-compression bonding technology, supports high-density heterogeneous integration and emerging applications such as high-performance computing, high-bandwidth memory and co-packaged optics. Our fluxless thermo-compression solutions support plasma and formic-acid vapor oxide-reduction capabilities, individually or in combination, to address different application requirements. We believe these capabilities position us to support increasingly complex assembly requirements associated with artificial intelligence infrastructure, including demand for greater bandwidth and power efficiency in data center and advanced networking applications.

[10-K FY2026｜anchor] Our major equipment competitors are ASM Pacific Technology
Significant competitive factors in the semiconductor equipment market include price, speed/throughput, production yield, process control, delivery time, innovation, quality and customer support, each of which contributes to lower the overall cost per package being manufactured. Our major equipment competitors are ASM Pacific Technology, Hesse GmbH, Han's Laser Technology Co., Ltd., BE Semiconductor Industries N.V., Hanwha Precision Machinery Co., Ltd., Hanmi Semiconductor Co., Ltd., and Nordson Corporation. Significant competitive factors in the semiconductor packaging materials industry include performance, price, delivery, product life, and quality. Our significant consumables competitors are PECO Co., Ltd., Disco Corporation, Small Precision Tools Co., Ltd. and Chaozhou Three-Circle (Group) Co., Ltd.

[10-K FY2026｜anchor] Approximately 56.3% and 53.5% of our net revenue for fiscal 2026 and 2025, respectively, was for shipments to customers headquartered in China
Our customer base in the Asia/Pacific region has become more geographically concentrated over time as a result of general economic and industry conditions and trends. Approximately 94.0% and 90.5% of our net revenue for fiscal 2026 and 2025, respectively, was for shipments to customer locations outside of the U.S., primarily in the Asia/Pacific region. Approximately 56.3% and 53.5% of our net revenue for fiscal 2026 and 2025, respectively, was for shipments to customers headquartered in China. The shipments to customers headquartered in China are subject to heightened risks and uncertainties related to the respective trade and export control policies of the governments of China and the U.S. Furthermore, there remains a potential risk of conflict and instability in the relationship between Taiwan and China that could disrupt the operations of our customers and/or suppliers in both Taiwan and China and our manufacturing operations in Taiwan and China.

[10-K FY2026｜anchor] The increase in inventories was due to the buildup of long lead time materials
The net change in operating assets and liabilities was primarily driven by an increase in inventories of $26.1 million after excluding the impact of the inventory write-down of $31.5 million, a decrease in income tax payable of $14.2 million and an increase in prepaid expenses and other current assets of $2.3 million. The increase in prepaid expenses and other current assets was mainly due to higher prepayments to suppliers. The decrease in accounts and other receivable was mainly due to lower sales for the year. The net increase in accounts payable, accrued expenses and other liabilities was primarily due to higher accrued employee termination benefits and adverse purchase commitments.
