[ABG Sundal Collier 分析師 Simon Granath 提問]
"As usual, I'd like to start on the OCS topic and ask for further color as how you see demand into 2027, especially on visibility on growth with your largest customer, but also to understand if the new OCS customers, which we know are in development phase, are already now contributing meaningfully to the figures."

[Edvard Kälvesten 回答]
"As you know, Simon, we are not guiding going forward into next year, but what we can say that the demand is high. We are continuing to deliver in the production for the biggest customer, big customer who is in production. The only OCS customer who really is in production in the world today. But also in addition, the 10 development customer we have are in different phases, and we have good hopes that some of them will transfer into production in next year."

[Edvard Kälvesten，瑞典擴產]
"So that is continuing the expansion of the cleanroom in Sweden with increasing capacity 15%-20%, and up to 35% additional when that is up to speed, that fab."
