Note（Proposed Merger with Marvell）：
"As further described in our Current Report on Form 8-K filed on November 2, 2026, on November 1, 2026, we entered into an Agreement and Plan of Merger (the “Merger Agreement”) with Marvell Technology, Inc. (“Marvell”), Java Merger Sub, Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Marvell (“Merger Sub I”), and Java Merger Sub II, Inc., a wholly owned subsidiary of Marvell (“Merger Sub II”)."
"At the Effective Time, each share of our common stock, par value $0.001 per share (“Common Stock”), that is issued and outstanding immediately prior to the Effective Time, subject to certain exceptions in the Merger Agreement, will be converted into the right to receive 4.500 shares of Marvell’s common stock, par value $0.002 per share of Marvell (“Marvell Common Stock,” and such ratio, the “Exchange Ratio”), with cash in lieu of fractional shares of Marvell Common Stock payable in accordance with the Merger Agreement."
"The completion of the Merger is subject to customary conditions, including, among others, adoption of the Merger Agreement by our stockholders, approval of the share issuance and charter amendment by Marvell’s stockholders, the expiration or termination of the waiting period under the Hart-Scott-Rodino Antitrust Improvements Act of 1976, as amended, and the receipt of certain other required regulatory approvals."
