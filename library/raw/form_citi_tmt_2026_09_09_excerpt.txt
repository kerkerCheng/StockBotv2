I know you are more in the insertion one and probably something in two, but from your perspective, are you seeing there is more challenges than you previously expected when you took on this challenge? Mike Slessor CEO, FormFactor Yeah. Well, being a bottleneck is a double-edged sword, right? It is a good place to be because it means you are relevant and you have chosen to solve a problem that the industry needs.
