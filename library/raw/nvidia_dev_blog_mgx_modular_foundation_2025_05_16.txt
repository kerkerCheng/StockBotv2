「Standardized building blocks」段——冷卻元件："The MGX Coldplate provides efficient liquid cooling for GPUs, maintaining optimal operating temperatures."
同段："The MGX 44RU Manifold manages coolant distribution within the rack."
同段："Quick disconnects, such as the MGX NVQD (NVIDIA quick disconnect) and MGX UQD (Universal quick disconnect), enable fast and secure connections for liquid cooling lines, simplifying maintenance and minimizing downtime."
「Why modular architecture matters now」——多源："Diverse sourcing options within the MGX ecosystem minimize investment risks, shorten lead times, and reduce uncertainty by allowing flexible selection of components and avoiding vendor lock-in. By enabling partners to choose from a broad array of certified components, MGX empowers organizations to optimize their data center builds for cost, performance, and supply chain resilience."
開頭段落——功率密度與冷卻："MGX addresses power density and cooling demands with liquid-cooled busbars and manifolds that enable a coolant temperature differential of less than 15°C even under 1400A loads, supporting up to 120 kW per rack for NVIDIA Blackwell GPUs."
