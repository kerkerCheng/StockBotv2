[EXCERPT — 有限逐字引文] Credo 新聞稿（2023-11-29）〈Credo Introduces World’s First 800G DSP for Linear Receive Optics, Targeting Hyperscale and AI Data Centers〉：Dove 850 為 LRO（亦稱 HALO）設計；LRO 只有發送路徑含 DSP
DATE: 2023-11-29
RETRIEVED: 2026-10-20（原檔本機抽字、空白攤平後逐字核對）
NOTE: DSP 廠自己對 LRO 架構的描述；2023 年稿，只撐「LRO 依賴發送端 DSP」這條下一層邊，不撐任何供貨。

【第一段（Dove 850 定位）】
"optimized for Linear Receive Optics (LRO), also known within the industry as Half-retimed Linear Optics (HALO)."

【第一段（LRO 的定義）】
"In an LRO transceiver or Active Optical Cable (AOC), only the transmit path from the electrical input to the optical line side output includes a DSP for signal retiming and equalization."
