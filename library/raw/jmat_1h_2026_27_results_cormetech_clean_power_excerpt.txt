[1H 2026/27｜Cormetech 與 Clean Air Solutions（Clean Power）]
On an annualised basis, the combined business is now expected to achieve sales in excess of £250 million (previously in excess of £200 million) and an operating margin of at least mid -teens in 2026/27.
In the two months since completion, we have already secured our first joint business win with a leading manufacturer of heat recovery steam generators. Together, Cormetech’s SCR catalysts and JM’s oxidation catalysts will be supplied into the emissions control system for gas turbines used in data centres . This demonstrates the potential for our combined offering and strong commercial proposition , reflected in a c.$350 million orderbook which underpins sales to 31st March 2028.
（註 3）Represents Cormetech’s orders due for delivery to 31st March 2028. Previously $300m orderbook representing orders due for delivery in calendar years 2026 and 2027 (May 2026 guidance).

[1H 2026/27｜半年貢獻]
Cormetech contributed £33 million sales in the half.
Cormetech contributed £6 million to underlying operating profit

[1H 2026/27｜報告結構]
we are changing our reporting structure for full year 2026/27. To align with how the businesses will be managed, we will report Clean Air Solutions and Cormetech (both currently reported as part of Clean Air heavy duty) in a single separate segment – “Clean Power”. 2025/26 pro forma financials for Clean Power will be provided in advance of our full year 2026/27 results.

[1H 2026/27｜Clean Air 規模]
For 2027/28, Clean Air (including Clean Air Solutions) is expected to deliver sales of more than £2 billion (of which over 95% is already won) and an improved operating margin of 16 to 18%.
