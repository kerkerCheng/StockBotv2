[Highlights]
"• Net revenue of $1.26 billion • GAAP gross margin of 51.0%; Non-GAAP gross margin of 53.2% • GAAP operating margin of 28.5%; Non-GAAP operating margin of 42.3% • Forecasting second quarter of fiscal year 2027 revenue of $1.575 billion to $1.625 billion; non-GAAP operating margin of 45.0% to 46.0%; and non-GAAP diluted net income per share of $5.90 to $6.20."

[CEO quote — Michael Hurlston]
"Lumentum built on its strong momentum in the first fiscal quarter of 2027, delivering growth across all our businesses, with pump lasers and optical circuit switches (OCS) being the leading contributors. During the quarter, we secured our second major NPO/CPO win, backed by purchase commitments exceeding $5 billion. Additionally, our OCS demand visibility has expanded significantly in recent months, positioning optical switching to become a multi-billion-dollar annual revenue stream. Finally, we continue to see strength in our cloud modules business which is showing improvement on the gross margin line,"

"Looking ahead, we anticipate adding approximately $350 million in sequential growth in our second quarter, a figure greater than the quarterly revenue of the whole company just two years ago. Furthermore, we project our second quarter operating margin to exceed 45 percent."

[Fiscal First Quarter Highlights]
"Net revenue for the first quarter of fiscal year 2027 was $1.26 billion, with GAAP net income of $360.3 million, or $3.54 per diluted share. Net revenue for the fourth quarter of fiscal year 2026 was $1.01 billion, with GAAP net loss of $7.2 billion, or $84.65 per diluted share. Net revenue for the first quarter of fiscal year 2026 was $533.8 million, with GAAP net income of $4.2 million, or $0.05 per diluted share."
