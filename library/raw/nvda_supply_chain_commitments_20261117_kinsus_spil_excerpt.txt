[NVIDIA 新聞稿 2026-11-17｜anchor] Under a new agreement with NVIDIA, Kinsus plans to expand its advanced ABF package substrate manufacturing capacity dedicated to NVIDIA by more than 3.5x over current levels
Under a new agreement with NVIDIA, Kinsus plans to expand its advanced ABF package substrate manufacturing capacity dedicated to NVIDIA by more than 3.5x over current levels from its Youth campus in Taoyuan, Taiwan. Production is expected to begin in the second half of 2027, with the additional capacity targeted for the third quarter of 2028. This multiyear agreement builds on the companies’ existing supplier relationship and supports NVIDIA’s long-term supply planning for its accelerated computing platforms.

[NVIDIA 新聞稿 2026-11-17｜anchor] NVIDIA and Samsung Electro-Mechanics have amended their existing substrate supply agreement
“After more than a decade of partnership with NVIDIA, we are excited to continue building the future together.” NVIDIA and Samsung Electro-Mechanics have amended their existing substrate supply agreement to improve operational capabilities at existing factories in support of increased production of NVIDIA’s accelerated computing platforms. The amendment builds on the companies’ previously announced multiyear collaboration supporting substrate manufacturing in Sejong and Busan, South Korea, for NVIDIA’s compute and high-speed networking technologies.

[NVIDIA 新聞稿 2026-11-17｜anchor] NVIDIA is supporting Amkor’s expansion of advanced packaging capacity in Arizona and South Korea through multiyear agreements announced in July and October 2026, totaling nearly $3 billion
NVIDIA is supporting Amkor’s expansion of advanced packaging capacity in Arizona and South Korea through multiyear agreements announced in July and October 2026, totaling nearly $3 billion. The agreements include prepayments to help Amkor scale manufacturing capacity for NVIDIA’s long-term production needs. “Our agreements with NVIDIA support investments in Arizona and South Korea that will serve multiple generations of AI platforms,” said Kevin Engel, president and CEO of Amkor.

[NVIDIA 新聞稿 2026-11-17｜anchor] NVIDIA and SPIL are expanding advanced packaging operations in Taiwan under a multiyear agreement supported by multibillion-dollar funding from NVIDIA
“That long-term planning allows us to advance our packaging technologies and add manufacturing capacity as NVIDIA’s production needs grow.” NVIDIA and SPIL are expanding advanced packaging operations in Taiwan under a multiyear agreement supported by multibillion-dollar funding from NVIDIA. The expansion will increase contracted advanced packaging capacity dedicated to NVIDIA by mid-2029. The project includes new production lines with turnkey support spanning bumping, interposer wafer fabrication, assembly and testing. These capabilities support semiconductor products with demanding requirements for computing performance, bandwidth, power efficiency and system integration.

Those plans included an aggregate 2.5x increase in substrate capacity over three years, expanded advanced packaging, a doubling of semiconductor testing capacity from 2026 to 2027 and a 15x increase in SPIL’s optical-engine capacity by mid-2027, with aims for another doubling by the end of 2028. “Our strong and long-standing collaboration with NVIDIA has continued to develop in the area of high-performance IC package substrates,” said Koji Kawashima, president and chief executive officer at IBIDEN.
