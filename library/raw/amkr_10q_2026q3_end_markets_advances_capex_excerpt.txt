【10-Q MD&A：Net sales】
"The computing and automotive and industrial end markets both grew 37% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to 2025, primarily due to strength in datacenter and ADAS applications. The communications end market decreased 28% for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to 2025, primarily driven by timing for transfer of SiP programs from our factories in Korea to Vietnam and related preproduction activities."

"As of September 30, 2026, we had cash and cash equivalents and short-term investments of $2,376.2 million."
"We enter into customer advance payment agreements from time to time, some of which require standby letters of credit.
