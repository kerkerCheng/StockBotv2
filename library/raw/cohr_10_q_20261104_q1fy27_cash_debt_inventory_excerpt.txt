【MD&A — Results of Operations（three months ended September 30, 2026）】
"Revenues. Revenues for the three months ended September 30, 2026 increased 50% to $2,370 million, compared to $1,581 million for the same period last fiscal year. Revenues in the Datacenter & Communications segment increased $862 million, or 79%, driven by continued strong AI datacenter demand. The Communications business also benefited from increased demand for data center interconnect, scale across and traditional telecom applications. Revenue in the Industrial segment decreased $73 million, or 15%, primarily due to the divestitures of our aerospace and defense business on September 2, 2025 and our Munich, Germany business on January 30, 2026."
"Gross margin. Gross margin for the three months ended September 30, 2026 was $928 million, or 39% of revenues, compared to $579 million, or 37% of revenues, for the same period last fiscal year, an increase of 254 basis points. The increase was primarily driven by lower product input costs, manufacturing efficiencies and yield improvements within the Datacenter & Communications segment. In addition, gross margin benefited from pricing optimization initiatives and lower amortization expense across both the Datacenter & Communications and Industrial segments."

【MD&A — Liquidity and Capital Resources】
Operating activities:
"Net cash used in operating activities was $93 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026 compared to net cash provided by operating activities of $46 million for the same period in the prior fiscal year. The increase in cash used in operating activities was primarily driven by a significant increase in inventory levels to support higher revenue growth, resulting in increased use of working capital. This was partially offset by higher accounts payable and higher net earnings."
Investing activities:
"Net cash used in investing activities was $201 million for the three months ended September 30, 2026, compared to net cash provided by investing activities of $297 million for the same period in the prior fiscal year. The increase in cash used was primarily due to $672 million higher cash used to fund capital expenditures partially offset by $625 million net purchases of short-term investments."

【Other Liquidity】

The proceeds from the investment will be used to support research and development, future capacity and operations as we build out our manufacturing capabilities. 【Notes — Contract Liabilities】
"Payments received from customers are based on invoices or billing schedules as established in contracts with customers. Contract liabilities generally relate to payments received in advance of performance under the contract. Contract liabilities are recognized as revenue when the performance obligations have been satisfied. During the three months ended September 30, 2026, we recognized revenue of $ 15 million related to customer payments that were included as contract liabilities in the Consolidated Balance Sheet as of June 30, 2026. We had $ 333 million of contract liabilities recorded in the Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheet as of September 30, 2026. As of September 30, 2026, $ 172 million of contract liabilities is included within Other accrued liabilities, and $ 161 million is included within Other liabilities on the Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheet. As of June 30, 2026, $ 48 million of contract liabilities is included in Other accrued liabilities, and $ 15 million is included within Other liabilities on the Condensed Consolidated Balance Sheet."

【Notes — Equity（NVIDIA 私募）】
"On March 2, 2026, the Company entered into a Securities Purchase Agreement (the “Purchase Agreement”) with NVIDIA Corporation (“NVIDIA”), pursuant to which the Company issued and sold 7,788,161 shares of Common Stock at a price of $ 256.80 per share, for aggregate gross proceeds of $ 2 billion."
"The shares issued to NVIDIA were subject to a contractual lock-up for a period of six months following the closing date, during which NVIDIA could not, without the Company’s prior written consent, directly or indirectly transfer or otherwise dispose of the shares or enter into transactions that transfer the economic risks or benefits of ownership."
"The price protection provision expired in September 2026 without being triggered. Accordingly, no additional shares were issued and no cash settlement was required."
