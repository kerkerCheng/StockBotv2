Research excerpt:
- National Champions section names Agility Robotics ($CCXI at lead time; Agility Robotics
  going public via Churchill Capital Corp XI SPAC merger, ticker AGLT post-close) among the
  top-10 US humanoid robotics companies, alongside Figure AI, Apptronik, Tesla, Boston
  Dynamics, Physical Intelligence, 1X Technologies, Amazon Robotics, Covariant, Skild AI.
- Digit is described as the only humanoid currently generating revenue from productive
  commercial work, citing 100,000+ totes moved at GXO.
- RoboFab manufacturing capacity is cited at 10,000 units/year; Agility has raised $641M.
- Challenges section frames rare earth exposure (neodymium for motors, samarium-cobalt for
  high-temp applications; alternative sourcing described as nascent) and actuator dependency
  (series elastic actuators, quasi-direct-drive motors, precision reducers overwhelmingly
  sourced from Japan, Germany, China) as structural vulnerabilities for the US humanoid sector.
- Citing Goldman Sachs data, China controls roughly 26% of the global actuator market versus
  ~5% for the US; China also controls ~60% of global rare-earth production.
- Report projects at least two significant consolidation events (acquisition or merger) among
  US humanoid companies in 2027, given at least six well-funded competitors in an early-formation market.
