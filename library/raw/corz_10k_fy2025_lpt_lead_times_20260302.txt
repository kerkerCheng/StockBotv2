【Item 1 Business（長交期設備）】
"We believe the procurement of long-lead-time electrical and mechanical equipment has become among the most significant factors affecting delivery timelines for large-scale AI infrastructure deployments. Certain components, including high-voltage transformers, switchgear, generators and advanced cooling systems, can require lead times of 12 to 18 months or longer, which can materially affect data center construction schedules."
