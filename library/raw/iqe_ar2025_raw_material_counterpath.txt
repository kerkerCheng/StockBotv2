Principal risk 8 - Supply of raw materials (p.47). Context: Geopolitical tensions have resulted in a specific risk for IQE relating to the supply of critical compound semiconductor raw materials such Gallium, Indium, Antimony and Germanium, including substrates incorporating those materials. Risk: Supply chain disruption due to the imposition of export controls and/or global supply shortages. Possible impact: Inability or delay in servicing customer orders; manufacturing disruption due to supply shortages, potentially resulting in reduced revenue; delay in revenue realisation. Mitigation: Engagement with relevant government bodies and alternative material suppliers, specifically suppliers outside of China.
