【CEO 引言：在手與管道】
"The continued visibility we have in our $2.2 billion backlog – the vast majority of which is permitted and underway, our rapidly growing $9 billion sales pipeline, and our productivity and synergy initiatives reinforce my confidence in our strategy and my conviction in our ability to deliver significant long-term value creation."

【2026 全年指引更新】
"Raising full year Orders to exceed $2.5 billion, compared to previous outlook of at least $2.0 billion"
"The projects in our backlog remain largely on schedule, and our sales pipeline continues to expand in both size and quantity. While we anticipate additional orders to be realized in power markets, I would emphasize that demand for our environmental, industrial and thermal solutions remain equally robust and often are realized later in the project supply chain cycle and generally represent funded, active projects rather than speculative demand,"

【2027 全年展望】
"The Company introduces its 2027 full year outlook with revenue expected to be between $2.1 billion and $2.3 billion, representing growth of more than 60 percent at the midpoint compared with the midpoint of the Company’s 2026 revenue outlook, and Adjusted EBITDA between $475 million and $525 million, representing growth of more than 130 percent at the midpoint compared with the midpoint of the Company’s 2026 Adjusted EBITDA outlook."
