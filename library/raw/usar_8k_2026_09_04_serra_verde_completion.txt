[EXCERPT — 有限逐字引文] USA Rare Earth 8-K Exhibit 99.1（2026-09-04 新聞稿）：完成與 Serra Verde 的合併
RETRIEVED: 2026-10-17（EDGAR 原文經 fetchers.edgar 抽字、空白正規化後逐字核對）

"today announced the completion of its combination with Serra Verde Group (“Serra Verde”) on September 3, 2026"
"Serra Verde is the only scaled producer of all four magnetic and other critical heavy rare earth elements outside Asia."
"The first stage of this program is expected to reach a run-rate of approximately 4,000 tons per annum (tpa) of total rare earth oxide (TREO) production by the end of 2026."
"Construction is underway on the second stage of the expansion, targeting average production of 6,400 tpa of TREO, with commissioning expected to begin within 12 months."
