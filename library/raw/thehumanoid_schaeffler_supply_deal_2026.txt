Schaeffler will become Humanoid's preferred supplier, covering more than 50% of the company's demand for joint actuators for its wheeled-based platforms through 2031. Humanoid provides the robotic systems together with the related services required for end-to-end deployment and operation; four-digit number of wheeled units across Schaeffler's global facilities by 2032.
