Title: Applied Optoelectronics, Inc. (AAOI) Q2 2026 Earnings Call Transcript
Call date: 2026-08-06 (Q2 FY2026 results)
Retrieved: 2026-08-11

Research excerpt (executive quotes):
- CFO Stefan Murry: "We just can't make enough of them to be involved in their current
  first-generation deployments because there's just not enough capacity."
- CFO Stefan Murry: "We have to prioritize our ability to make lasers for our own
  transceivers first, and then as we expand the fab... then we'll have more capacity for
  the ESAP, for the CPO type laser."
- CFO Stefan Murry: "Forecast demand for 800G and 1.6 terabit modules are projected to
  continue to exceed our production capacity through mid-2027."
- CEO Thompson Lin: customer demand is "20%-40% higher" than current capacity.
- CEO Thompson Lin, on Chinese CPO laser competitors: "If you're talking about reasonable
  year and no quality issue or performance issue, I would say easily at least two, three
  years or even longer."
- CFO Stefan Murry, on ELS/ELSFP ramp: "We have very limited production of these modules
  now, but we anticipate ramping production later this year and into 2027, ultimately
  culminating in about 400,000 pieces per month in 2028."
- Q2 2026 results: revenue $191.9M (record, +86% YoY, +27% QoQ), non-GAAP EPS $0.06 beat.

Boundary: this is AAOI's own management commentary (self-report), not independent
third-party confirmation. The "China 2-3 years behind" assessment is forward-looking
management judgment, not a filed fact, and management has an incentive to frame Western
laser positioning favorably — scored as guided/lower-confidence, not confirmed. No claim
is made here that this benefits SIVE, LITE, AVGO, or COHR specifically; that inference
was in the originating social posts but is not stated by AAOI management and is not
asserted as a graph claim.
