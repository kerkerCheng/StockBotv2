[CEO prepared remarks — integrating new material into SiPho]
"Longer term, we are building the capability to integrate new material into our SIFO platforms to further expand our served available market."
"In one such example, we announced initial production for new photonics with a new technology where we integrated Indian phosphide lasers within our silicon photonics dye."
"This is done through a dyed-a-wafer attach of Indian phosphide epi-chiplets further processed in our factory to create a final dye that incorporates Indian phosphide lasers within our silicon photonics."
"As this technology ramps, we gain access to the laser market and additional multiples of increase in serviceable market."

[Q&A — Craig-Hallum, laser attach rate; CEO answer]
"I truly do not have a clear answer to it."
"Our lead customers there are very, very committed for growth with very strong forecasts."
"And again, those are numbers that are not yet within the model, but they can be very appreciable, especially as one could imagine, there are appreciably higher ASPs against a very good baseline to begin with."
