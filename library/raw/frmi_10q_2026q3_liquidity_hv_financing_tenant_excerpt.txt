【Note 5 高壓設備融資：額度】
"High Voltage Equipment Financing On February 19, 2026, Fermi High Voltage Warehouse LLC (“HVW”) entered into a master loan agreement (the “Keystone Master Loan Agreement”) with Keystone National Group, LLC, as agent, and Keystone Private Income Fund, as initial lender, providing for equipment-backed advances of up to $120,000 in aggregate principal, with the potential to increase by an additional $100,000 subject to lender approval (the “High Voltage Equipment Financing”)."

【Note 5 高壓設備融資：撥款與餘額】
"As of September 30, 2026, HVW had received advances totaling $94,691 under two promissory notes, each of which bears interest at a fixed rate of 12.90% per annum and matures on August 19, 2031. During the three months ended September 30, 2026, HVW made the first two scheduled principal installments totaling $3,156. As of September 30, 2026, $91,535 was outstanding under the High Voltage Equipment Financing."

【Note 5 高壓設備融資：不能再撥】
"As of September 30, 2026, no further advances were available to HVW under the High Voltage Equipment Financing."

【Note 5 高壓設備融資：條款】
"The Keystone Master Loan Agreement includes a minimum liquidity covenant of $20,000, a mandatory prepayment requirement if an approved customer agreement has not been received by December 31, 2026, and a collateral coverage requirement under which outstanding principal may not exceed 110% of the fair market value of the financed equipment."
