【Oracle 投影片〈Lessons learned from GW-scale AI deployments〉p.9〈1.6T: Linear Receive Optics (LRO)〉（Mark Filer，Oracle；pypdf 抽字）：1.6T 改用 LRO、多家 3nm DSP 互通、LRO DSP 2nm】
"LPO cannot comfortably meet SI and interop performance requirements at 1.6T; LRO provides the best low power solution currently available: • 26dB channels at 16W power consumption (10 pj/bit) • Performs equally well over short, medium, long reach channels • Supports DR4+ (4dB), and FR4 links in the upper tiers • Interop has been demonstrated from different suppliers and multi-vendor 3nm DSPs • More aggressive power reduction expected as LRO DSP technology matures in 2nm."
