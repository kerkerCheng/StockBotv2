Exercise of Warrants, Issue of Equity and TVR

IQE plc (AIM: IQE, "IQE" or the "Group"), the leading global supplier of compound semiconductor wafer products and advanced material solutions, announces that Mark Cubitt, Non-Executive Chair, has given notice to exercise warrants held over 1,709,803 ordinary shares of 1 pence each ("Ordinary Shares") in the capital of the Company at an exercise price of 13.5 pence per share.

Following the exercise of the warrants, Mark Cubitt will hold 3,005,108 Ordinary Shares representing 0.23% of the issued share capital.

Admission and Total Voting Rights

Application has been made to the London Stock Exchange for Admission of the 1,709,803 Ordinary Shares to trading on AIM ("Admission"). It is expected that Admission will become effective on or around 27 October 2026.

The Ordinary Shares will be issued fully paid and will rank pari passu in all respects with the Company's existing Ordinary Shares.

Following Admission, the total number of Ordinary Shares in the capital of the Company in issue will be 1,332,837,130 with voting rights. This figure may be used by shareholders as the denominator for the calculations by which they will determine if they are required to notify their interest in, or a change to their interest in, the Company's issued share capital.

ABOUT IQE

IQE is the leading global supplier of advanced compound semiconductor wafers and materials solutions. IQE's technologies enable high-performance devices across a diverse range of end markets including:

· AI & datacentre

· Consumer electronics

· Communications infrastructure

· Aerospace and defence

· Automotive and industrial

IQE combines more than 35 years of specialist expertise with a broad technology portfolio and a global manufacturing footprint across the UK, US and Taiwan, supporting customers from innovation through to high-volume production.

IQE is headquartered in Cardiff, UK, and is listed on the AIM-market of the London Stock Exchange.
