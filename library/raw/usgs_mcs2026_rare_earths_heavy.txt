USGS Mineral Commodity Summaries, February 2026 — RARE EARTHS (HEAVY). Net import reliance as a percentage of apparent consumption, compounds and metals: 100 (2021-2025). Substitutes are available for some applications but are generally less effective. Light rare earths can substitute for heavy rare earths in several applications.
