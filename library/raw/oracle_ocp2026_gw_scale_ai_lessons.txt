【p.6〈800G: Linear Pluggable Optics (LPO)〉】
Addressing TCO at 800G, OCI has large deployment of LPO transceivers in AI scale-out networks

【p.6 資料集】
Plots show 24-hr max pre-FEC BER across identical T0<>T1 links, 156k LPO and 202k FRO in data set(359k total)

【p.7〈800G: Linear Pluggable Optics (LPO) II〉】
FRO links experienced 1.75x more transitions than LPO

【p.9 標題】
1.6T: Linear Receive Optics (LRO)

【p.9】
LPO cannot comfortably meet SI and interop performance requirements at 1.6T; LRO provides the best low power solution currently available

【p.9 互通】
Interop has been demonstrated from different suppliers and multi-vendor 3nm DSPs

【p.9 結論】
Takeaway: LRO is viable at 1.6T and markedly improves network TCO for AI scale-out applications
