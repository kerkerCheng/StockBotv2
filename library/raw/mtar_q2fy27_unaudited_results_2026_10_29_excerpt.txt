【合併說明】
"Subsequent to the period end, on October 28, 2026, the Hon'ble National Company Law Tribunal ("NCLT"), Hyderabad Bench-II has approved a Scheme of Amalgamation ("Scheme") of Gee Pee Aerospace and Defence Private Limited and Magnatar Aero Systems Private Limited (wholly owned subsidiaries) with the Company with effect from April 01, 2025."
"As stated in note 2 above, both the subsidiaries have now been merged with the Company as per the Scheme. Accordingly, no consolidated financial statement is required to be prepared by the Company."
"The Company's business activity falls within a single line of business segment, in terms of Ind AS 108 "Operating Segments"."
