PUBLISHED: 2026-10-21（NVIDIA Newsroom 新聞稿署日）
RETRIEVED: 2026-10-23（本機抓 HTML 轉文字、逐字核對）

【Press release body（Bolt 段）】
Bolt has selected NVIDIA Alpamayo Stack for its European robotaxi program, beginning with future Lucid vehicles built on Hyperion.

【Press release body（Bolt 段）】
Bolt plans to begin gathering real-world driving data to support the development and validation of the autonomous driving technology — subject to regulatory approval — in Madrid in 2027, with Germany being a priority for future deployment as part of a planned European rollout of at least 25,000 level 4-capable future Lucid vehicles built on NVIDIA Hyperion.

【Press release body（Volkswagen 段）】
Hyperion provides a common computing and sensor architecture designed to simplify integration and validation, helping developers and mobility operators bring robotaxi and commercial transport services to market.
