【七段】
TrendForce identifies seven major segments in the CPO testing supply chain: ATE, probing and test interfaces, optical and high-speed measurement instruments, test integration and automation, core optical coupling and alignment modules, third-party testing and analysis, and packaging and production testing.

【ATE（Teradyne）】
Teradyne acquired Quantifi Photonics in 2025 to strengthen its optical measurement capabilities.

【ATE（Advantest）】
Advantest is integrating optical instruments through its existing test platforms and software. In September 2026, the company announced a collaboration with VIAVI and SENKO to combine automated testing, optical measurement, and detachable optical interfaces.

【對準與探測】
GMT Global provides multi-axis optical coupling and alignment modules, while MPI offers probing equipment and optical alignment solutions.

【介面與燒機】
WinWay specializes in test interfaces, and Chroma has capabilities in automated testing and burn-in testing.

【第三方實驗室】
Third-party testing providers, including MSS, MA-tek, and iST, are helping customers accelerate product development through optical-electrical measurements, materials analysis, failure analysis, and reliability verification.

【ficonTEC】
RoboTechnik completed its full acquisition of Germany-based ficonTEC in 2025, gaining a foothold in the global optoelectronic packaging and test equipment supply chain.

【瓶頸是測試速度】
a complete test of a single photonic integrated circuit (PIC) can still take over 100 seconds. Consequently, the bottleneck in mass production is now shifting from alignment accuracy to testing speed.

【時程】
TrendForce expects 2027 to be a pivotal year for the convergence of CPO testing architectures and equipment investment strategies, while 2028 will serve as a critical period for validating readiness for large-scale mass production.
