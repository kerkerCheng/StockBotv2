【前言】
"They do not create new legal requirements; determine whether any specific transaction, equipment, country, person, or vendor is covered; or prejudge future DOE actions."

【1. What does Executive Order 14421 do?】
"Directs DOE to publish implementing rules or regulations within 120 days, as needed."

【4. What equipment may be within the scope of the executive order?】
"Substation transformers, reactors, capacitors, voltage regulators, circuit breakers, protective relays, meters, and related equipment."
"Associated critical components, software, firmware, digital services, maintenance services, remote access capabilities, lifecycle update mechanisms, and supply-chain dependencies may also be considered when they are associated with covered equipment and could present the risks identified in the executive order."

【DOE 徵詢的選項（節錄）】
"A categorical rule for equipment containing foreign cells, semiconductors, power electronics, software, or other components."

【19. How can the public submit comments or information to DOE?】
"Responses to the RFI are due October 9, 2026."
