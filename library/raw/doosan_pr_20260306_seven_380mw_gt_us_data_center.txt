[EXCERPT — 有限逐字引文] Doosan Enerbility 新聞稿（2026-03-06，斗山集團官網）：與美國客戶簽 7 台 380 MW 燃機，2029-05 起每月各交一台燃機與發電機給客戶興建中的資料中心；在美累計 12 台
RETRIEVED: 2026-10-27（本機原文抽字逐字核對）

【導言】
"On March 6th, Doosan Enerbility announced that it had signed a contract with a U.S. client to supply the company with seven units of its 380MW gas turbine."

【第 2 段】
"Starting in May 2029, Doosan is to sequentially deliver gas turbines and generators, one of each per month, to the data center being built by the client."

【第 2 段】
"With this recently won contract, Doosan Enerbility now has a total of 12 gas turbines to deliver in the United States."
