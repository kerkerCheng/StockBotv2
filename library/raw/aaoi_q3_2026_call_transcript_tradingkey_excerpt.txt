【財務長 Stefan Murry，準備稿：長約條款】I'd like to share some additional details about this agreement. The customer is an existing customer and the LTA covers 1.6 terabit transceiver products exclusively. We recently successfully qualified our first 1.6 terabit product with this customer. Subject to certain conditions, including our ability to supply, this customer has agreed to purchase a minimum of 10% of its required 1.6 terabit transceivers from AOI over the 3-year period from June 2026 through May 2029. We currently expect this LTA to result in between $1 billion and $1.5 billion in revenue during this time period, although it could vary from this amount significantly depending on the customer's actual demand for 1.6 terabit products. In turn, AOI has committed to supplying this volume and to agreed pricing, including some price reductions over the period. The customer made no commitment of capital as part of this LTA.

【財務長 Stefan Murry，準備稿：800G 低於預期的原因、第四季原料、2027 年需求】While 800G growth was strong, it was somewhat below our expectations primarily because our LTA customer shifted its demand from 800G to 1.6 terabit more quickly than we had anticipated. We expect this shift to continue in Q4. We expect our fourth quarter data center revenue to be between $270 million and $300 million, representing 281% year-over-year growth at the midpoint. While we continue to see exceptionally strong demand for our data center products, we expect fourth quarter data center revenue to be somewhat below our earlier expectations because, as I just mentioned, our LTA customer is shifting its orders from 800G to 1.6 terabit faster than we had anticipated. We believe this shift is positive for us in the long term because 1.6 terabit carries higher pricing and margins than 800G. However, the accelerated timing has left us without sufficient 1.6 terabit raw materials in Q4 to fully meet the increased demand. As we expected, this customer returned as a greater-than-10% customer in Q3, and we expect it to remain one throughout the term of the LTA. Looking ahead, we expect demand for 800G and 1.6 terabit modules to exceed our production capacity throughout 2027. We are continuing to add capacity and to secure the key components needed to meet this demand.
