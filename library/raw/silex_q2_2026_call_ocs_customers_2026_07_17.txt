【Silex Microsystems Q2 2026 法說（2026-07-17）SEB 分析師問 OCS：CEO「in the range of 10 OCS customers in total and one in high volume production」、公開所知真正規模部署只有一家、「the only MEMS foundry producing OCS」（Investing.com 第三方逐字稿經 r.jina.ai 取原頁 markdown，tier 2，未對錄音；發行人自述，L8 弱；客戶未具名）】
"Just wanted to follow up on the OCS, please. Can you say roughly how many customers do you have on optical circuit switches right now? How many of these are in development and how many are in production? I’ll start there. Edvard Kälvesten, CEO and Founder, Silex Microsystems: We don’t give these exact numbers, but in the range of 10 OCS customers in total and one in high volume production. Erik Lindholm-Röjestål, Analyst, SEB: Just in general, do you have any sense as to the penetration of optical circuit switches in data centers generally right now? It’s still mainly one hyperscaler that has deployed this, right? Edvard Kälvesten, CEO and Founder, Silex Microsystems: As of my knowledge is, what is communicated on the market publicly, it’s only one who have that in real scale. Some others are together with some of Silex customers and maybe some are not Silex customers, testing also. I think they have some way to go. The leader seems to be a good leader. Erik Lindholm-Röjestål, Analyst, SEB: Yep. All right. Are there any OCS customers that you don’t have, so to speak? Edvard Kälvesten, CEO and Founder, Silex Microsystems: Everybody’s asking that. We don’t share that. What I can say is that we have all of them, or most of them as customers, or we are speaking with most of them to be a customer. Erik Lindholm-Röjestål, Analyst, SEB: Okay. Edvard Kälvesten, CEO and Founder, Silex Microsystems: As the only MEMS foundry producing OCS and having done that for 10 years now, we are a natural player to work with if you want to develop an OCS chip very fast and to a cost-efficient way."
