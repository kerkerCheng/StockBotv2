[EXCERPT — 有限逐字引文] Accelleron 2026 半年報：High Speed 增壓器營收年增 40%，由美國資料中心燃氣主電源帶動、靠引擎 OEM 擴產支撐；柴油備援成長受 OEM 產能分配限制；資料中心相關營收占比由約 5% 升到略低於 9%；瑞士廠接近滿載
RETRIEVED: 2026-10-27（本機原文抽字逐字核對）

【Company financial and business review】
"High Speed: produces and services turbochargers with power outputs ranging from 0.5 to 5 MW, for the use of one to four turbos per engine."

【Letter to shareholders】
"Growth was driven by continued strong demand for turbochargers used in gas-fired prime power applications for data centers in the U.S., supported by capacity expansions at key engine OEMs."

【Letter to shareholders】
"Revenue growth in diesel-fired backup power applications was constrained by OEM capacity allocation."

【Letter to shareholders—Outlook】
"The manufacturing capacity of engine OEMs will be the key growth driver going forward."

【Letter to shareholders—Outlook】
"While we benefit from the additional growth from data center applications, the absolute volumes are still limited in relation to total revenue."

【Letter to shareholders—Outlook】
"In the first half of 2025, around 5% of our revenues were data center-related. In the first half of 2026, this figure increased to slightly below 9%."

【Letter to shareholders—Raised guidance】
"Since the stock listing in 2022, Accelleron's business volume has almost doubled, bringing its Swiss manufacturing operations close to capacity limits."
