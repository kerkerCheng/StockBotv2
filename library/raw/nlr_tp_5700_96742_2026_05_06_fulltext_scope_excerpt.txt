【Executive Summary（擴產仍不足；資料中心是需求來源之一）】
Domestic manufacturing capacity has expanded in recent years, with facility upgrades announced by Delta Star, Hitachi Energy, and Pennsylvania Transformer and new capacity planned by Siemens Energy. Yet these expansions remain insufficient to meet rising demand from hydropower, data centers, and renewable integration.

【LPT costs（生產者物價指數）】
Transformer costs have increased sharply since 2021, with prices in 2025 approximately 80% higher than the relatively constant value between 2014 and 2021 (Figure 8).
