"(Hong Kong, 28 October 2026) – ASMPT Limited (ASMPT / the Group / the Company) (Stock code: 0522) ... announced its unaudited results for the three months ended 30 September 2026 (Q3 2026)."
"Group Bookings at a record high of HK$8.85 billion (US$1.13 billion), up 24.7% QoQ and 137.9% YoY, with book-to-bill ratio of 1.69, highest since Q1 2021."
"AI-related demand also continued to support the SEMI mainstream business, particularly in power management and AI server-board applications for data centres. Revenue and bookings remained elevated, with wire bonding and die bonding tools supported by AI infrastructure expansion and high utilisation in OSATs."
"The Group expects Q4 2026 revenue to be between US$670 million and US$730 million, up 4.6% QoQ and 37.5% YoY at mid-point of US$700 million, which is above the market consensus. This will be driven by double-digit SMT revenue growth, while SEMI revenue is expected to be flat due to longer lead times of AP products."
